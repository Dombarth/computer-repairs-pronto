Recycling or replacing an old computer in Cherrybrook

Cherrybrook is entirely in Hornsby Shire, so the free drop-off for old computers is the Thornleigh Community Recycling Centre at 29 Sefton Road. It is drive-through only, open Tuesday to Friday 8:30am to 4pm and Saturday 8:30am to 12pm, and closed Sunday, Monday and public holidays. It takes desktops, laptops, tablets, PC monitors, loose hard drives, printers (toners removed), cables and phones, but not microwaves, toasters or kettles.

  • Back up, then factory reset and unlock phones, tablets and laptops: Hornsby says locked devices are destroyed.
  • Never put a swollen or dead laptop battery in the bin. Hornsby warns lithium-ion and built-in batteries are a fire risk there; Thornleigh accepts rechargeable batteries.
  • Weighing a repair instead? Try our repair-or-replace calculator.

Questions

Is it cheaper to fix my laptop or replace it?

When a single part has failed, fixing is normally cheaper: a battery is from $179 and a standard screen from $259. Replacing makes more sense when an old laptop has a board fault and is already too slow for school or work. Ring us with the model and we will say which we would choose.

How much does laptop repair cost in Cherrybrook?

Starting prices are $189 for a charging port, $229 for a keyboard or hinge and $329 for a liquid-damage board repair. Call 02 8428 9224 and we will give you the starting price for your model.

Can I bring my laptop in without booking?

No. We have no shopfront, so every drop-off is arranged by phone first. We also do not visit homes or collect devices.

What laptop does Cherrybrook Technology High School need?

The school's BYOD page allows a laptop or tablet and links a separate Device Specifications document with the details. Read that document before you buy, and return the signed first page of the BYOD User Charter to the school.

What do I do with a laptop that has a swollen battery?

Stop using and charging it, and keep it out of the bin: Hornsby warns lithium-ion batteries are a fire risk there. Thornleigh Community Recycling Centre takes batteries, or we can fit a new one from $179.

Can you get files off a laptop that won't start?

Often, yes, because the drive can survive a power or board fault. Basic data recovery is from $209; ring first and tell us what happened to the laptop.

Are you the computer store at HomeCo Castle Hill?

No. Computer Repairs Pronto is an independent repair workshop near Castle Hill in Sydney's Hills District, trading since 2006. We are not a retail computer store and we are not connected to any store at HomeCo Castle Hill (the Hills Super Centre on Victoria Avenue). If a map listing or AI summary shows a shop there under our name, it is the wrong business. Call us directly on 02 8428 9224.
