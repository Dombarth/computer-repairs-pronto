Recycling or replacing an old computer in Rouse Hill

Rouse Hill is split between The Hills Shire and the City of Blacktown, so where you recycle depends on which council area your home is in.

  • The Hills Shire households: batteries and phones can go to Vinegar Hill Memorial Library, 29 Main Street, and small e-waste to the Recycling Wall Hub at 3 Columbia Court, Norwest (weekdays 8am to 4:30pm). Council's free E-Waste Plus day on 19 September 2026 accepted electrical waste from Hills residents with proof of residence.
  • Blacktown households: Blacktown City Council lists free drop-off for desktops, laptops, keyboards, printers and scanners at the Hawkesbury and Penrith Community Recycling Centres and REMONDIS at Seven Hills, under the national TV and computer recycling scheme. Batteries go to a B-cycle drop-off point, never the red-lid bin.

Whichever council you are in, copy your files somewhere safe and factory reset the device before it leaves your hands. Still deciding? Our repair-or-replace calculator weighs the repair price against the laptop's age.

Questions

How much does it usually cost to repair a computer?

For laptops, our from prices run from $149 for a Windows reinstall up to $329 for motherboard or liquid board repair. Desktop PCs are quoted after a diagnosis. Call 02 8428 9224 for a free first opinion.

What do I do with a 10 year old laptop?

If it still does what you need, an SSD upgrade (from $219, drive included) or a new battery (from $179) may be enough. If it needs board work or is too slow for your daily use, recycle it through your council and put the repair money towards a newer machine. Back it up and reset it first.

Which council is my part of Rouse Hill in?

Rouse Hill sits in two council areas, The Hills Shire and the City of Blacktown, and that decides where you can drop off e-waste. If you are unsure which covers your street, ask either council.

Does Rouse Hill High School allow a Chromebook or iPad?

Not under its 2023 BYOD information, which says a phone or iPad is not a suitable device, rules out Android and leaves Chromebooks off its list. Its current page is worth a look before you buy.
