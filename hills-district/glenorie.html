nbn Fixed Wireless in Glenorie and working from home

Glenorie is on nbn's "Planned" list for Fixed Wireless upgrade works, but nbn warns that not all premises in listed suburbs will become eligible. nbn describes the list as indicative only, so check your own address on nbn's upgrade page. Where it applies, Fixed Wireless Plus reaches up to 100/20 Mbps, with faster Home Fast and Superfast tiers at select locations.

If your work laptop drags on video calls, run a speed test on a phone using the same Wi-Fi. A fast phone and a slow laptop point to the laptop, and a call to us costs nothing before you make the 20-minute drive.

Recycling or replacing an old computer in Glenorie

The Hills Shire lists no drop-off point in Glenorie itself, so old computers travel to Thornleigh or Seven Hills.

  • Thornleigh Community Recycling Centre, 29 Sefton Road, is free for any NSW household and drive-through, open Tuesday to Friday 8:30am to 4pm and Saturday mornings until noon. It takes laptops, desktops, tablets, monitors, printers with toners removed and household batteries.
  • Hills Shire side: REMONDIS at 29 Powers Road, Seven Hills, accepts PCs, laptops, monitors and printers, with a 15-item limit per visit.
  • Small items: Hills household batteries and phones go to Dural Library or the Community Environment Centre on Currie Avenue, Annangrove (outside, open any time). Hornsby also points to B-cycle drop-off points at supermarkets and retailers.

Copy off your photos and documents, then factory reset the computer before it leaves the house. Unsure whether to recycle or repair? Try our is it worth fixing calculator.

Do you visit homes in Glenorie?

What should I do with a 10-year-old laptop?

A laptop that old usually cannot run Windows 11 officially, and Windows 10 no longer gets security updates. If it only does light jobs, an SSD (from $219) can make it usable again; otherwise copy your files off, reset it and recycle it at Thornleigh or Seven Hills.

Will my laptop be fixed the same day?

We do not promise it. Some jobs can be done the same day when the part is in stock, so ask when you ring.
