Recycling or replacing an old computer in Carlingford

Carlingford residents can drop old computers, laptops and phones free at the City of Parramatta Community Recycling Centre, 104 Grose Street, North Parramatta. Opening hours are 8am to 4pm Tuesday to Friday and 8am to noon on Saturday; it is shut on Sunday, Monday and public holidays.

  • E-waste is taken from City of Parramatta residents only, up to 5 items a day, and the centre is not for commercial use. It does not take vapes.
  • Household and car batteries are taken from anyone.
  • E-waste must not go in the red-lid or yellow-lid bin. The council also lists the Cleanaway centres at Auburn and Ryde for computers and TVs.
  • Recycling Near You lists Carlingford Branch Library for batteries and phones, but the listing is undated and the council's own page leaves it out, so ask at the desk first.

While your laptop is in for repair

Carlingford Branch Library at 17 Lloyds Avenue has free Wi-Fi and public computers, and stays open until 8pm on Wednesdays. On other weekdays it opens 9:30am to 5pm, and on Saturday 9:30am to 12pm. A library card gives you up to 90 minutes a day on the computers; a guest ticket gives 15. A4 printing is 10c black and white or 50c colour (EFTPOS only), and scanning to email or USB is free.

Questions

How much should a PC repair cost?

Laptop starting prices run from $149 for a Windows reinstall to $329 for a no-power motherboard repair. A desktop PC is quoted once it has been diagnosed; the $150 diagnostic is waived if you go ahead. Call 02 8428 9224 with the make, model and fault for free advice first.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace a computer?

Usually repair, when one part has failed on a machine that still does the job, such as a $179 battery or a $229 keyboard. On an old, slow computer, a major board repair can cost more than it is worth. Should the diagnostic show it is not worth fixing, the $150 is all you pay, and we explain why.

Does Carlingford High School accept Chromebooks?

No. Its BYOD page supports Windows 8, 10 and 11 and macOS only, and lists Chromebooks, iPads, Android devices and Linux as not supported. The page is undated, so confirm with the school.

Do you do house calls in Carlingford?

No. We do not do onsite visits, call-outs, pick-ups or courier. Every repair is dropped off at the workshop by arrangement, after you call.

Is a same-day laptop repair possible?

Some jobs can be done the same day when the part is in stock. Ring before you drop it off so we can check.
