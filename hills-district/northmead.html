Recycling or replacing an old computer in Northmead

Northmead is in the City of Parramatta, so its residents can take an old computer to the council's free Community Recycling Centre at 104 Grose Street, North Parramatta. The part of Northmead that was in The Hills Shire moved across on 12 May 2016.

  • The centre accepts PCs, laptops, monitors, phones, TVs and consoles from Parramatta residents, up to 5 items a day. Hours: Tuesday to Friday 8am to 4pm, Saturday 8am to 12pm.
  • For computers, the council's e-waste list also gives Officeworks Auburn and the Cleanaway centres at Auburn and North Ryde.
  • Battery drop-offs: ALDI at 37-39 George St, Parramatta, and Remondis at 29 Powers Rd, Seven Hills, which also takes mobile phones.
  • Red-lid and yellow-lid bins are not for e-waste.

Questions

How much does it normally cost to repair a laptop?

With us, common laptop jobs start at $159 for a fan and thermal service, $179 for a battery, $229 for a keyboard or hinge and $259 for a standard screen. Liquid damage on the board starts at $329. Call 02 8428 9224 with the model and fault for a free steer before you bring it in.

Should I fix my old laptop or buy a new one?

Fix it if one part has worn out and the laptop still suits you. Replacing often makes more sense when the board has failed on a slow, ageing machine. Phone advice is free, and if our $150 diagnostic shows it is not worth fixing, we tell you why.

Do you repair MacBooks for Westmead school students?

Yes. We repair MacBooks and iMacs, although PC laptops and desktops are our main work. Ring with the model for a quote.

Is there a counter I can walk into?

No. We have no walk-in counter or shopfront. Drop-off is by arrangement, after you phone us.

How long is the warranty?

It runs for 12 months and covers both parts and labour if the same fault reappears.
