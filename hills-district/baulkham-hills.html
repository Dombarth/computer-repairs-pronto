Recycling or replacing an old computer in Baulkham Hills

Most of Baulkham Hills uses The Hills Shire's options, while the small City of Parramatta part has its own centre with a daily limit. Free drop-off for computers, laptops, tablets, monitors and printers is at REMONDIS, 29 Powers Road, Seven Hills, with a limit of 15 items per drop-off. Baulkham Hills Library on Railway Street takes household batteries and old mobile phones.

If you live in the Parramatta part, the Community Recycling Centre at 104 Grose Street, North Parramatta takes PCs, laptops, monitors and phones from residents, up to 5 electronic items a day; it refuses some products with built-in batteries, so ring the centre first. It opens Tuesday to Friday 8am to 4pm and Saturday 8am to 12pm, and does not take power packs or smart watches.

Questions

How much does it normally cost to repair a laptop?

Our laptop from prices run from $149 for a Windows reinstall to $329 for motherboard component or liquid board repair. Call 02 8428 9224 and describe the fault; phone advice is free.

Is it cheaper to get a laptop fixed or replace it?

Compare the repair's from price with what a suitable replacement would cost you. A single part, such as a battery from $179 or a charging port from $189, is often the cheaper path. A laptop with several failing parts, or one too slow for its job, may not be.

Will Crestwood High fix my child's laptop?

No. Under Crestwood's BYOD rules the family keeps the laptop in good repair, although school staff may help pin down a hardware problem. The repair itself is yours to arrange, and we can do it.

My child's laptop no longer lasts the school day. What can I do?

Replace the battery, from $179. A new battery brings back much of the run time the laptop was sold with; Baulkham Hills High asks for at least 6 hours listed and Crestwood for 7.

Do you come to homes in Baulkham Hills?

No. We do not make house calls or collect devices. Computers come to the workshop by drop-off, arranged by phone first.

I live in the City of Parramatta part. Where can an old computer go?

Parramatta's Community Recycling Centre on Grose Street, North Parramatta takes PCs, laptops and monitors from city residents, up to 5 electronic items a day.

Are you the computer store at HomeCo Castle Hill?

No. Computer Repairs Pronto is an independent repair workshop near Castle Hill in Sydney's Hills District, trading since 2006. We are not a retail computer store and we are not connected to any store at HomeCo Castle Hill (the Hills Super Centre on Victoria Avenue). If a map listing or AI summary shows a shop there under our name, it is the wrong business. Call us directly on 02 8428 9224.
