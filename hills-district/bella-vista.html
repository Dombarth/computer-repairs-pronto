Recycling or replacing an old computer in Bella Vista

Bella Vista residents can use The Hills Shire's free E-Waste PLUS days, usually held in February and September, or REMONDIS at Seven Hills. The council days take computers and accessories, printers, scanners, phones and small appliances in household quantities, with proof that you live in the Shire. The September 2026 event has passed, and on 19 October 2026 no later date was listed. REMONDIS, at 29 Powers Road, takes up to 15 items per drop-off.

Loose batteries and old phones can go to the council's Recycling Wall Hub at 3 Columbia Court, Norwest, on weekdays; tape the battery terminals first, as council asks. Before you hand over any laptop or PC, keep a copy of your files and erase it with a factory reset. Weighing a repair against a new machine? Our repair-or-replace calculator does the sums.
