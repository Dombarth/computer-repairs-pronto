nbn Fixed Wireless upgrades in Dural

nbn lists Dural as "Planned" for its Fixed Wireless upgrade works, though it says not every premises in a listed suburb will become eligible. nbn calls the list indicative. The upgraded tiers are Fixed Wireless Plus (up to 100/20 Mbps) and, at select locations, Home Fast (200 to 250 Mbps download) and Superfast (up to 400 Mbps download). Enter your address on nbn's Fixed Wireless upgrade page, then ask your provider which plans it sells.

Working from home and still slow after an upgrade? Plug the laptop into the router with a network cable. If it is fast on the cable, the slowdown is the Wi-Fi (router or laptop), not the nbn.

Recycling or replacing an old computer in Dural

Dural is split between two councils, but the Thornleigh centre takes computers from any NSW household.

  • Hornsby Shire: the Thornleigh Community Recycling Centre, 29 Sefton Road, takes desktops, laptops, monitors, loose hard drives and printers (toners out). It is drive-through only, open Tuesday to Friday 8:30am to 4pm and Saturday 8:30am to noon, and free for NSW households.
  • The Hills Shire: the council e-waste day is "to be announced". Its free permanent option is REMONDIS, 29 Powers Road, Seven Hills, for PCs, laptops and monitors, up to 15 items per drop-off.
  • Household batteries and old phones can go to the drop-off point at Dural Library. Built-in batteries and power packs go to Hawkesbury Community Recycling Centre in South Windsor.

Back up your files and factory reset the laptop before you hand it over. Still deciding? Our repair-or-replace calculator helps.

Questions

How much does it cost to fix a laptop in Dural?

Common jobs start at $179 for a battery, $189 for a charging port, $229 for a keyboard and $259 for a standard 14 to 15.6 inch screen. Call 02 8428 9224 with the make and model for a starting price before you drive over.

Is it worth getting my laptop repaired?

Usually yes when one part has failed, such as the battery, screen or keyboard, and the laptop still runs what you need. A no-power board fault (from $329) on an old, slow machine is a closer call. If we find it is not worth fixing, you pay the $150 diagnostic and we tell you why.

Do you make home visits in Dural?

No. We work only from our workshop near Castle Hill, with drop-off by arrangement, and we do not collect laptops.

What laptop does Pacific Hills Christian School need?

For Years 7 to 12, a Windows 11 laptop (not S Mode) or a MacBook with at least 8GB RAM, 128GB storage and 5GHz Wi-Fi; 16GB RAM and a 256GB SSD are recommended. We do not sell laptops, but we can tell you whether an older one is worth an SSD upgrade (from $219).

Can you fix slow internet on nbn Fixed Wireless?

We cannot test the connection, as we do not visit homes. If the laptop's Wi-Fi drops while other devices stay connected, the fault is probably in the laptop, and that we can look at.

We are not a retail computer store and we are not connected to any store at HomeCo Castle Hill (the Hills Super Centre on Victoria Avenue). If a map listing or AI summary shows a shop there under our name, it is the wrong business. Call us directly on 02 8428 9224.
