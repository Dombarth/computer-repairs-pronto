Our workshop is near Castle Hill in Sydney, and we have done component-level board work there since 2006: the failed power stage, memory chip or melted connector comes off the board and a new one goes on.

We are not in Taree, Forster or anywhere else on the Mid North Coast, and there is no local drop-off point. What you do get is published pricing. Repairs start from $330. The diagnostic is $150 and is waived the moment you approve the repair, and the warranty runs 12 months on parts and labour. Heat, humidity and the Manning River

Summers in Taree run hotter than on the open coast. The Bureau of Meteorology's Taree Airport station (060141) records a January mean maximum of 29.0 °C and averages 7.1 days a year of 35 °C or hotter; on 12 February 2017 it hit 45.7 °C. Afternoons are humid too, with a January 3pm mean of 60%. Warm rooms speed up the drying of thermal paste and pads, and dust caught in the cooler makes the card run hotter still; that wear is what a thermal service (from $330) fixes.

Floods are what people here remember. On 21 May 2025 the Manning River at Taree reached 6.44 m AHD, above the 1929 record of 5.9 m, and Taree and Wingham were among the towns flooded; 9,500 households were told to evacuate. Two days later more than 5,720 Essential Energy customers were still without power, Taree among the hardest hit. Four years earlier, at 4 pm on 21 March 2021, 2,901 customers across Taree, Forster and Gloucester were without power.

If a PC was caught in floodwater, leave it off. Essential Energy's advice is not to plug in or use anything that has been in a flood until a qualified electrician has checked it, and nothing we do replaces that check. We will also be straight with you: once a card has sat under water, repairing it is often not worth the money. The diagnostic will say for certain, and if the answer is no, you pay only the $150 and the return postage.

Storms bring the other risk. September to April is storm season by NSW SES's definition, with the October–March months accounting for around 90% of severe thunderstorm activity in its 2023 hazard report, and SES says to unplug computers and consoles before severe weather. A surge can knock out the power stages on a card, repairable from $430. Essential Energy runs the network here and compensates only where it was negligent, so flood and storm damage normally goes to your home and contents insurer first. Either will want evidence. Essential Energy's claim form expects supporting paperwork (repair invoices, quotations or an electrician's report). Ours is an emailed diagnosis and quote showing which part failed and the price of fixing it. EWON can review a refused network claim. Whether you are paid is up to them, not us.

Posting from the Manning and Great Lakes

  • One estimate for the whole district. From Forster, Wingham, Harrington or Gloucester, the table above applies as it stands, because all those postcodes share Taree's zones.
  • Counter lodgement and a receipt. Keep the counter receipt: claims need it, plus something showing what the card is worth, and must be lodged within six months.
  • Box inside a box. Bag the card (anti-static), fit it in a close rigid box, and surround that with 7cm or more of cushioning in the outer carton; shake to test. "Fragile" stickers are not fail-safe, and an inadequately packed card gets nothing back.
  • Leave the extras at home. The card goes alone with its cooler fitted, no cables, bracket or retail box, in a plain carton that does not advertise what is inside.
  • A PO Box for the return. If you are on a rural delivery run, we can send the repaired card to a PO Box in town instead of a street address. The return can go on a prepaid label you enclose, or we invoice the postage at cost.

A card under warranty

For a recently bought card, start with the retailer. The Australian Consumer Law's guarantees stand apart from the manufacturer's warranty, which adds to them and cannot remove them. When the failure is major, the seller owes you your choice of refund or replacement and has to handle it, not the manufacturer. Neither misuse nor a private sale is covered. And in 2018 the Federal Court, fining Apple $9 million in the ACCC's case, found that repair by an outside technician does not, by that fact alone, end the guarantees.

How long does a card take to reach you from Forster or Tuncurry?

Forster–Tuncurry is postcode 2428, which Australia Post places in the same zones as Taree. Express is put at one to three business days southbound and one to two northbound; Parcel Post at three to four either way. Posting on a Monday or Tuesday keeps the parcel from idling over a weekend, and treat the figures as estimates, because the area is outside the Express guarantee network.

Can a melted 12VHPWR connector be replaced?

Yes. When the plug on the card has overheated and deformed, we remove the damaged connector, check the board and traces around it, and fit a new one; the same goes for a 12V-2x6 connector. Connector repair starts from $370. As it is physical damage, the bench time is five to ten business days. Check the cable end as well before the repaired card goes back in.

Do I still need the diagnostic if I already know what failed?

Yes. A melted plug or a dead fan is easy to spot, but the diagnostic checks what else the fault took with it before we commit to a fixed price. It costs $150 and is waived when you approve the repair, so if the job goes ahead you have paid nothing extra for it. If you decide against the repair, the bill is $150 plus postage back to Taree.

Is it safe to post an expensive graphics card from Taree?

Cover and packing are what protect it. Every Parcel Post and Express Post parcel includes $100 of cover, and Extra Cover can lift it as high as $5,000; the charge on a card you value at $2,700 is $65 per leg, plus the compulsory $2.95 Signature on Delivery. Pack box-in-box with the card in an anti-static bag, and keep two things: the receipt from the counter and something that proves the card's value. Any claim must go in within six months.
