Highland frosts, wind storms and your card

Cold sets Bowral apart. The Bureau of Meteorology's Bowral (Parry Drive) station, with records from 1961 to 2014, averaged 51.6 nights a year at or below 2 °C and 24.7 at or below freezing, with a record low of −11.2 °C in July 1971. Hardware brought in from a cold car or unheated shed can gather condensation, so let it reach room temperature before powering up.

Summers are gentler: a January mean maximum of 25.5 °C, and only 18.3 days in an average year hit 30 °C. Even so, heat and long gaming sessions dry out paste and pads over the years, and dust clogs the fins. Either shows up as a card running hot in a cool room, a thermal service job from $330.

Wind is the bigger power risk. Damaging winds on 2 September 2024 cut supply to more than 44,000 Endeavour Energy customers, with some of the worst lingering damage in Moss Vale, Bundanoon, Mittagong and Exeter; on 1–2 July 2025, torrential rain and wind left more than 17,000 without power, the Southern Highlands among them. NSW SES treats September to April as storm season, and its list of sensitive appliances to unplug before severe weather starts with "computers, gaming consoles"; Endeavour agrees. Should a surge reach the PC, the card's power stages are at risk, and replacing them starts from $430.

Endeavour Energy, which runs the Highlands network, warns that claims may be declined for severe storms and lightning, so a home and contents policy is normally where a claim begins. Our emailed diagnosis and quote record what failed and the cost to fix it, the paperwork insurers and networks tend to want, though no outcome is ours to promise. EWON can review a network's refusal.

Packing a card for the trip up from the Highlands

  • Warm first, then box. On a frosty morning, give the card time indoors before packing, and again when it returns.
  • Box in a box. The inner box should be snug and rigid, the outer one cushioned, as Australia Post recommends. Sendle's version: anti-static bag first, padding at least 7cm deep, then shake it. A "Fragile" sticker is no safeguard.
  • Plain carton, bare card. Aramex's tip is an unbranded box, nothing that says electronics. The card travels with its cooler on and the job number inside; cables, brackets and retail packaging stay home.
  • Receipt, proof of value, return plan. An Australia Post claim needs both documents within six months, and fails if the packing was inadequate. For the return, enclose a prepaid label or have postage invoiced at cost, to a street address or PO Box.

Before you post: check a courier's cover before choosing it on price. Sendle includes $100 and sells more only up to $1,500, well short of a $2,600 card, and CouriersPlease's standard cover does not extend to electronic devices at all.

The full packing guide, including the photos to take first and water-cooled cards, is on our mail-in repair page.

A new card goes back to the seller first

Before paying for any repair on a recently bought card, go to the retailer or use the maker's warranty. The Australian Consumer Law treats a warranty as an extra layer on the consumer guarantees, never a replacement. Faced with a major failure, you pick the remedy, refund or replacement, and the seller must handle it rather than point you at the manufacturer. Misuse and private purchases fall outside. In ACCC v Apple (2018, $9 million in penalties), the Federal Court held that third-party repair alone does not end the guarantees; a maker's own warranty terms are another matter.

My gaming PC sits in a cold room over a Highlands winter. Is that a problem?

The risk we can speak to is condensation. The Bowral station averaged about 52 nights a year at or below 2 °C, and hardware carried from a freezing car or shed into a heated room can collect moisture on the board. Give the PC or card time to reach room temperature before switching it on. Do the same with a repaired card that arrives back from us on a frosty morning, before it goes into the machine.

Can you repair a melted 12VHPWR connector?

We can, on 12VHPWR and 12V-2x6 cards alike. It counts as connector work, priced from $370, and if you go ahead the $150 diagnostic is deducted. If the card is a write-off, what do I pay?

Just the $150 diagnostic and, if we post the card back, the return postage. There is no other charge.
