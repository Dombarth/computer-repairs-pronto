Border summers, storms and substations

Albury summers are hot and dry. The Bureau of Meteorology's Albury Airport AWS gives a January mean maximum of 32.5 °C; on average 71.2 days a year reach 30 °C, 21.4 reach 35 °C and 3.4 reach 40 °C, and January humidity at 3pm averages just 28%. The station's record, 46.1 °C, was set on 4 January 2020, beating the 45.3 °C of 16 January 2019.

A graphics card is cooled by the air in the room, so in a hot room it runs hotter. Over the years its paste and pads dry out; the card gets hotter still, the fans work harder, and eventually it throttles or crashes mid-game. Dust in the fins adds to it. The fix is a thermal service, priced from $330.

Heat strains the network too. In the record heatwave of 25–31 January 2026, with daily peaks as high as 45.1 °C, Essential Energy had outages three nights running in North Albury, Glenroy and Lavington, because substation equipment could not cool down as it normally would and fuses blew. On 18 January 2023, thunderstorm conditions cut power to as many as 2,844 homes and businesses in North Albury. NSW SES and Essential Energy both say to unplug the PC before a storm; SES storm season runs September to April. A surge that gets through can kill the power stages, and replacing those starts from $430.

On the NSW side the network belongs to Essential Energy, which pays out only where it was negligent and says storm damage is generally not reimbursed; its claim paperwork calls for invoices, quotes or an electrician's report. Wodonga is on AusNet's network. On either side, start with your home and contents insurer. Our emailed report and quote put the failed part and the repair price in writing, the kind of evidence insurers and networks ask for, though nobody can promise the outcome. EWON reviews refused NSW claims.

Five things to get right when posting from the border

  1. Keep it out of a hot car. Go straight from home to the counter on a 40-degree afternoon, and post early in the week rather than on a Friday, so it spends less time in a depot over a weekend.
  2. Box inside a box. Australia Post's advice is a rigid inner box that fits snugly, cushioning, then an outer box. Sendle: bag the card against static, surround it with at least 7cm of cushioning, and shake-test the box. A "Fragile" label will not do that job.
  3. Plain outside, card only inside. Aramex suggests packaging that does not advertise electronics. Pack the card, cooler fitted, with the job number; cables, bracket and retail box stay home.
  4. Cover it for the distance. Keep the lodgement receipt and proof of value; claims close after six months, and nothing is paid on an inadequately packed item. With Sendle, $100 of cover comes included and extra cover stops at $1,500, below a $2,200 card; CouriersPlease's included cover excludes electronic devices.
  5. Say where it goes back to. A street address or PO Box in Albury, Wodonga or nearby, with a paid return label packed alongside the card, or return postage invoiced at cost.

Water-cooled cards and the rest of the packing detail are covered on our mail-in hub.

Warranty and consumer rights come first

If the card is recent, use the retailer or the warranty before paying for repair. The Australian Consumer Law guarantees stand regardless of any warranty, which can only add to them. For a major failure, you pick refund or replacement, and the seller, rather than the manufacturer, must provide it. Misuse and privately bought goods are excluded. Ordering $9 million in penalties against Apple in the ACCC's 2018 case, the Federal Court held that repair by someone other than the maker does not, on that ground alone, cancel the guarantees. Manufacturer warranty terms can still differ.

Does Albury's summer heat damage graphics cards?

Heat dries out thermal paste and pads over time, and a card with tired paste runs hotter, throttles and can crash in games. Albury Airport averages 21.4 days of 35 °C or more a year, so a PC in an uncooled room works its cooler hard through summer. Dust in the heatsink makes it worse. A thermal service, from $330, replaces the paste and pads and clears the cooler.

What will a repair cost me from Albury?

Is there anywhere in Albury that repairs graphics cards?

In our September 2026 search, no Albury-Wodonga business published a graphics card repair service or a GPU repair price. Shops on both sides of the border offer upgrades, meaning a new card fitted, along with general PC and phone repairs. They are a sensible first stop for a driver, software or power supply problem. For board-level work on the card itself, posting it to us is the realistic option.

Can a graphics card be repaired, or does it need replacing?

Plenty of faults are repairable at component level: a burnt power stage, a failed memory chip, a melted 12VHPWR connector, corrupted firmware or dried-out paste. Whether it is worth doing depends on the card. A recent GeForce (RTX 30 onwards) or Radeon (RX 6000 onwards) usually is. One worth a couple of hundred dollars used rarely justifies the diagnostic and the postage from Albury.
