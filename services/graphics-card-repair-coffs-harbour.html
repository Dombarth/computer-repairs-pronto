Coffs Harbour weather and the faults it leaves behind

Coffs Harbour is the only NSW location in the table of a Bureau of Meteorology lightning paper (Dowdy and Kuleshov, 2014), which gives it a total lightning flash density of 9.9 per km² per year for 1995–2012, counting cloud and ground flashes together rather than ground strikes. At about 2pm on 20 October 2021 a supercell dropped golf-ball-sized hail; a Toormina shopping centre roof caved in, and the SES fielded nearly 800 calls the next day, with Boambee, Bonville, Sawtell and Toormina among the suburbs hit.

A surge that slips past the power supply can kill the power stages feeding the graphics chip; often the fans twitch then stop, or the PC will not boot. Power stage repair is from $430. NSW SES reckons on storms from September through April, and on its figures about 90% of the severe thunderstorm activity lands in October–March. It and Essential Energy advise pulling a computer's plug ahead of severe weather.

Essential Energy, which runs the local network, limits compensation to losses caused by its negligence and counts adverse weather as beyond its control, so a storm-damage claim normally begins with your home and contents insurer. Both ask for evidence like a repair quote or an electrician's report; our emailed diagnosis names the failed part and the repair cost. Payment is their call, and EWON, the NSW energy ombudsman, can review a refused network claim.

Coffs Harbour MO (station 059040, 1943–2015) logs 1,702.4 mm of rain a year and 69% humidity at 3pm in January (1943–2010), with a January mean maximum of 27.0 °C and only 1.2 days a year reaching 35 °C. Heat slowly dries thermal paste and pads and dust clogs coolers, so fans roar at light load and clocks sag mid-game; a thermal service, from $330, is the cure. Humid, salty coastal air is a general hazard for electronics too. July nights average 7.6 °C; a card that comes home cold should warm to room temperature before power-up, or condensation can form.

Floods happen here too: on 23 November 1996 up to 300 mm fell in two hours, flooding about 260 homes and 200 commercial buildings above floor level, and one person died. Leave a flood-soaked PC unpowered until a licensed electrician has been over it, as NSW SES and Essential Energy advise; our bench does not replace that check. Cards that have been under water are frequently past saving, and the diagnosis will say which way yours falls.

Getting the parcel out of Coffs Harbour

  • Counter receipt. No guarantee to protect on this route, but the receipt is your proof of lodgement for any cover claim.
  • Skip the beach car park. A closed car in January is no place for a boxed card.
  • Box inside a box. Australia Post: a snug, rigid inner box, cushioning, then the outer box. Sendle: anti-static bag, 7 cm or more of cushioning, and a shake test. "Fragile" labels do not do the work.
  • Just the card, in plain cardboard. Cooler on; leads, brackets and retail box stay home; job number inside. Aramex suggests a box that does not say electronics.
  • Sort the return now. Put a prepaid return label inside, or we bill return postage on the invoice at cost. PO Box returns are fine.

Recently bought cards go back to the shop first

A card young enough to be under warranty should go back to its seller first. Consumer guarantees stand whatever the warranty says. The seller handles the claim, not the maker, and a major failure means refund or replacement at your choice; misuse and private sales fall outside. The Federal Court found in 2018 (ACCC v Apple) that independent repair does not by itself cancel those guarantees, though the maker's own warranty terms are a separate matter.

Questions

Can a graphics card killed in a Coffs Harbour storm be repaired?

Often. When a surge gets through the power supply, the card's power stages can be the casualty, and replacing them is from $430; the graphics chip may have come through untouched. Test the card in a second PC if one is handy, because the same surge can also kill a power supply or motherboard. Tell us about the storm on the form. Our emailed diagnosis doubles as a record of what failed if an insurer asks.

Does Express Post from Coffs Harbour arrive the next day?

No, not on Australia Post's figures. From 2450, in the NSW Rural zone, Express to our Sydney Metro postcode is put at two to three business days, and the return leg the same. Parcel Post runs three to four each way. The next-business-day guarantee applies only inside the Express network, which 2450 is not part of, so a slow Express parcel earns no refund. On the estimates, Express still saves about a day per leg.

How much is a graphics card repair from Coffs Harbour, all in?

The $150 diagnosis only stays on the bill if the repair does not go ahead. Then postage both ways, which you choose; a card you value at $3,500 needs $85 of Extra Cover per leg and the compulsory $2.95 signature.

Is there board-level graphics card repair in Coffs Harbour?

What do I pay from Coffs Harbour if my card cannot be saved?
