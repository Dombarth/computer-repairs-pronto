Posting tips for the South Coast

  • Straight to the counter on hot days. Nowra averages 6.9 days a year at or above 35 °C. On days like that, take the parcel directly to the Post Office rather than leaving it in a parked car. A Monday or Tuesday lodgement also means less chance of a weekend in a depot.
  • Keep the lodgement receipt. A counter lodgement gives you the proof an Australia Post claim needs, with proof of value, within six months. Poor packing voids a claim.
  • Two boxes and a shake. Australia Post's advice is a snug and rigid inner box, then a cushioned outer box. Sendle puts the card in an anti-static bag, calls for 7cm of cushioning at minimum, and wants a shake before sealing. A "Fragile" label will not protect it on its own.
  • Nothing on the outside to tempt anyone. Aramex recommends a plain box. Inside goes the card with its cooler fitted and your job number, and nothing else.
  • Street or PO Box return. Enclose a prepaid return label or have return postage put on the invoice at cost. A PO Box is fine if it is easier to collect from.

Worth knowing: couriers differ on cover. Sendle's price covers $100, with top-ups available to $1,500, a little under the $1,600 example, and CouriersPlease's included cover excludes electronic devices. For the step-by-step packing guide, see the mail-in hub.

Retailer first for a newer card

New cards bought from an Australian business carry consumer guarantees, and the maker's warranty sits on top without subtracting anything. When the failure is major, you choose a refund or a replacement, and the seller has to deal with it rather than sending you to the manufacturer. Misuse and private sales are excluded. The 2018 Federal Court case ACCC v Apple, which ended in $9 million of penalties, found that third-party repair does not by itself end the guarantees. The maker's warranty terms are their own matter.

How many business days does the post take between Nowra and Sydney?

By Express, Australia Post estimates one to three business days from Nowra 2541 up to our Sydney Metro postcode, and one to two for the trip home. Can a Shoalhaven storm surge kill a graphics card?

Yes. If a surge reaches the PC, the power stages on the card are what it can take out, leaving a card that is dead or shuts down under load. Replacing them is repair work from $430. Before storms, NSW SES and Endeavour Energy both advise unplugging computers. If a surge has already struck, leave the PC off and let the diagnostic establish what failed; the written result can support a claim on your home and contents insurance.

What are the warning signs that a graphics card is failing?

Watch for artefacts such as coloured squares, streaks or corrupted textures, which often point to memory; fans that spin to full speed before the card dies; no display at all; crashes or black screens once a game loads; and temperatures that climb faster than they used to. A driver, a cable or the monitor can cause several of these too, so run the checks in our artefacting guide before posting anything from Nowra.

Is my card worth repairing, or should I buy a new one?

Value decides it. Replacing dried paste and pads, from $330, is worth doing on most cards you intend to keep. Power stage or memory work pays off on mid-range models and above, especially NVIDIA's RTX 30, 40 and 50 lines and AMD's RX 6000 to 9000. On a card that would only resell for a couple of hundred dollars, two lots of postage from Nowra plus the $150 diagnostic usually point towards replacing it, and we will tell you so.
