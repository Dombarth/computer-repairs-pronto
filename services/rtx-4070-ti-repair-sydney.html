RTX 4070 Ti vs 4070 Ti Super: What Differs on the Board

The 4070 Ti Super is not a re-badged 4070 Ti: it uses a larger GPU, a wider memory bus and two more memory chips, so parts, firmware and test results are specific to each card. They share a 285W power rating, and every one of them is a partner-built card.

What that changes on the bench:

  • Firmware is not interchangeable. It is matched to the brand, model and board revision, not just the GPU name.
  • Memory testing follows the layout. Six memory channels on a 4070 Ti, eight on a Super, so errors are mapped to the right chip.
  • Power advice is the same. Same 285W rating, same 16-pin input, same 700W recommendation.
  • The GPUs cannot be swapped. AD104, AD103 and AD102 are different chips, and on either card a failed GPU is rarely worth replacing.

From "RTX 4080 12GB" to 4070 Ti Super: The Model History

The RTX 4070 Ti was announced in September 2022 as the "RTX 4080 12GB", withdrawn by NVIDIA that October, and released under its new name in January 2023; the Ti Super followed a year later. The dates tell you how old a card can be, and so whether it may still be under warranty.

The 12GB 4080 never went on sale. TechSpot and Tom's Hardware both noted that the 4070 Ti kept its core specifications; only the name and price changed.

Is Your 4070 Ti or Ti Super Still Under Warranty?

Check before paying for any repair: the 4070 Ti Super only went on sale in January 2024, so many are still inside their maker's warranty, and even an older 4070 Ti may still be covered by the consumer guarantees in the Australian Consumer Law.

  • 4070 Ti Super: on sale from 24 January 2024, so none is more than about two years and nine months old. Warranty periods differ by brand and region; compare your maker's terms with the date on your invoice.
  • 4070 Ti: on sale from January 2023. Many are now outside the maker's warranty, but your invoice date and the brand's terms decide it.
  • Consumer guarantees: the ACCC says the guarantee of acceptable quality usually still applies after a warranty expires. Those rights sit with the retailer that sold you the card.
  • Bought second hand: ask the seller for the original invoice; a claim is much harder without proof of purchase.

If the card is covered, go to the retailer before anyone works on it. If the claim is refused, we can diagnose it.

The 16-Pin Power Connector on 4070 Ti Cards

Both cards take all their external power through one 16-pin socket, and a scorched or melted socket can usually be replaced from $370, as long as the heat has not burnt into the board beneath.

  • NVIDIA's specification: a PCIe Gen 5 cable rated for 300W or more, or the adapter in the box, which takes two 8-pin plugs.
  • 4070 Ti: the cards reviewed by KitGuru and Igor's Lab use the original 12VHPWR socket.
  • 4070 Ti Super: some cards use the revised 12V-2x6 socket, others still use 12VHPWR; Tom's Hardware reported in February 2024 that some partners were still fitting the older one.
  • 12V-2x6 is PCI-SIG's revision with shorter sense pins, which Tom's Hardware reported as backward compatible with 12VHPWR.
  • Adapters differ: Palit's GameRock and Gigabyte's Gaming OC came with two-8-pin adapters; Zotac's 4070 Ti, Inno3D's iChill X3 and MSI's Suprim X with three-8-pin versions. KitGuru found the Inno3D performed the same on two plugs as on three.

The widely reported melting cases centred on the RTX 4090; in November 2022, when it knew of about 50 cases, NVIDIA said a common issue was connectors not being fully plugged in. A 4070 Ti draws far less power, but a plug that is not fully home, or a damaged pin, can still overheat at the socket.

  1. The socket and the board around it are inspected under magnification, and the 12V input is measured for a short.
  2. The damaged socket is removed, the pads and copper beneath are checked, and a new socket of the correct type is fitted.
  3. The power stages fed from that input are tested.
  4. The card runs at full power while the socket is checked with a thermal camera.

Replace the cable or adapter that was in use. A heat-damaged plug can ruin a new socket. If heat has burnt into the board's layers, you are told before any work starts, because the repair may not be economical.

RTX 4070 Ti GDDR6X Memory: Six Chips, or Eight on the Super

A 4070 Ti carries six 2GB GDDR6X chips and a 4070 Ti Super eight, all around the GPU on the front of the board, and a failed chip can be replaced on its own from $530, subject to the correct chip being available.

  • Layout: each chip has its own 32-bit slice of the bus; six make the 4070 Ti's 192 bits, eight the Super's 256.
  • Diagnosis: no two chips share a slice, so a memory error points to one chip. Cards that pair front and rear chips, such as the RTX 3090, need an extra step.
  • The chips: teardowns such as Hardware Busters' Galax 4070 Ti Super show Micron 2GB GDDR6X rated at 21Gbps, the speed both cards run at.
  • Cooling: the memory sits under the main cooler, so hot memory often points to pads that have shifted, compressed or been refitted at the wrong thickness.
  1. The memory is tested, warm where the fault only shows with heat, and errors traced to a chip.
  2. The memory supply is checked first. Igor's Lab counted two or three memory phases on the boards it examined, and a weak memory rail can look exactly like a failing chip.
  3. The chip is lifted with controlled heat and the board's pads cleaned and inspected.
  4. A new chip of the correct part is fitted. If it has to be ordered, the quote says so.
  5. All six or eight chips are retested, pads and paste renewed, and the card load-tested.

RTX 4070 Ti No Display

A 4070 Ti or Ti Super that spins its fans but shows nothing usually has a power rail that is not coming up, corrupted firmware or a fault on the output side, and most of these are repairable. Try these first:

  1. Push the 16-pin plug fully home and check every 8-pin plug on the adapter is connected.
  2. Try another output and another cable.
  3. If the card has a BIOS switch, power off, move it and try again. On the Gigabyte Gaming OC and MSI Suprim X, reviewers found the two positions differ only in fan behaviour, so nothing is lost.
  4. Check whether the motherboard or Windows sees the card. If it does, firmware, memory or the outputs are likely; if not, a rail that is not starting or damage at the PCIe contacts.

RTX 4070 Ti Power Stages and Short Circuits

A failed power stage on a 4070 Ti or Ti Super can be replaced from $430 with a part that matches that maker's design, because phase counts and components vary widely between boards. Power stages step the 12V input down to the voltages the GPU and memory run on, and a failed one often fails short.

  • Different parts on different cards: Igor's Lab found 55A Alpha and Omega stages on MSI's 4070 Ti Suprim X, with its power controllers on the back of the board; KitGuru counted 11 GPU phases on Inno3D's iChill X3 and 12 on Palit's GameRock.
  • Finding a short: with the card unpowered, each rail's resistance to ground is measured; a small, current-limited supply is then fed into the shorted rail and a thermal camera shows the part that warms.
  • Short on the 12V input: the power supply trips the moment the computer is switched on.
  • Short on a core or memory supply: fans may spin with a black screen, or the card is not detected.
  • The whole rail is checked: neighbouring stages, the controller and nearby capacitors, then the rail is brought up current-limited before full power and a sustained load.

If you suspect a short, stop powering the card. Each attempt can turn one failed part into several.

4070 Ti Crashes and Shutdowns Under Load

When a 4070 Ti crashes, or the whole computer switches off under load, the power supply and cabling are as likely a cause as the card, so check them first.

  • Power supply: NVIDIA lists 700W as the required system power for both cards.
  • Adapter wiring: run each 8-pin plug from its own cable, not both from one cable with two plugs.
  • Overclocked cards: some draw more than 285W; Igor's Lab found MSI's Suprim X could be raised to 365W.
  • Heat: watch GPU hot spot and memory temperatures in a tool such as HWiNFO.

If the card passes every bench test, you pay the $150 diagnostic and get the results.

4070 Ti Super Cards With an AD102 GPU

From mid-2024 some 4070 Ti Super cards were built on AD102, the RTX 4090's chip, cut down to the same 8,448 cores and 256-bit bus, so a Ti Super can carry either of two GPUs.

  • Which cards: Tom's Hardware reported Zotac's Solid OC and Gigabyte's WindForce Max OC with AD102 in July 2024, and later showed the Zotac's die marked AD102-175. Guru3D reported an AD102 "V2" from Colorful.
  • Performance: Tom's Hardware said it should bring no advantage, as only part of the chip is enabled, including four of its six memory controllers.
  • For repair: the GPU, the parts around it and the firmware differ. Tom's Hardware pointed to a different capacitor layout behind the GPU and wider-spaced cooler screws.
  • How to tell: GPU-Z shows the GPU name, or give us the model name from the label and we check before quoting.

How We Diagnose a 4070 Ti or Ti Super

Diagnosis starts by identifying the exact card and GPU, then works from harmless checks to invasive ones, so the fault is never made worse while it is being found.

  1. Identify the card: brand, model, board revision and GPU (AD104, AD103 or AD102).
  2. Inspect both sides under magnification, especially the 16-pin socket and power stages.
  3. Cold checks: the 12V input and every rail measured to ground, unpowered.
  4. Current-limited power-up, so a fault cannot draw enough to cause more damage.
  5. Rails, firmware and memory: each supply checked in start-up order, the BIOS chip read, and all six or eight memory chips tested.
  6. Quote: what failed, a fixed price and our view on whether it is worth doing. Nothing proceeds without your approval.
  7. Repair and test: component work, pads measured for that model, fresh paste, sustained load with temperatures logged.

Can a 4070 Ti Be Repaired?

Usually, yes: sockets, power stages, memory chips, small components and firmware can all be replaced, but a failed GPU core or a board burnt through its layers is rarely worth repairing.

  • Scorched or melted 16-pin sockets, where the board beneath is sound
  • Failed GDDR6X memory chips and power stages
  • Shorted or cracked capacitors and other small parts
  • Damaged display outputs and PCIe contacts
  • Corrupted or mismatched firmware

Usually not economical: a failed or cracked GPU core (a replacement would have to come from another card), heat damage through the board's layers, several memory chips plus a power fault, or pads torn by an earlier repair. You are told after the diagnostic, and pay only the $150 and any return postage.

Can an RTX 4070 Ti be repaired?

Do you repair the RTX 4070 Ti Super?
