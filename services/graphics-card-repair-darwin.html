Yes. Post the faulty graphics card (GPU) from Darwin to our Hills District workshop in Sydney and it is repaired at component level. We find the part that failed, such as a burnt connector, a dead power stage or a faulty memory chip, and replace it rather than the whole card. Plainly put, we are in Sydney, not the Territory. The card spends several business days in the post each way; this page is about planning around that.

Heat, the wet and lightning: the Darwin fault list

The Bureau of Meteorology gives Darwin mean maximums of 32.8°C in December, 31.8°C in January and 31.5°C in February. The wet season runs from October to April with humidity constantly above 60 per cent, 3pm readings of 65 to 72 per cent from December to February, and an average of 432mm of rain in January alone.

Heat first. A cooler can only pull a card towards room temperature, so a room above 30°C leaves little headroom, and paste and pads tend to dry out faster when they spend years hot. The card then throttles, runs its fans flat out, or crashes under load. A thermal service (from $330, one to two bench days) strips the old paste and pads, cleans the cooler and fits fresh ones.

Humidity is slower and less direct. A running card is warmer than the air, so it does not usually gather moisture in use. Trouble comes when damp settles into dust in the cooler, when a PC sits switched off through weeks of the wet, or when a cold card from an air-conditioned room meets humid air and condensation forms. Corrosion from any of these shows up under magnification during the diagnostic, and where it has damaged a port or trace, that repair starts from $370.

Lightning is the sudden one. Darwin Airport records an average of 13.4 lightning flashes per square kilometre per year, most of them from November to April. A surge that reaches the PC can burn out the card's power stages, a repair from $430, and may take the power supply with it, so check that too. Power and Water does not compensate for lightning or storm damage, so the bill is yours. Unplugging the PC when a storm builds is the cheapest protection there is.

Packing a card for the trip out of the Top End

  • Seal it in dry air. Bag the card in an air-conditioned room so the air sealed in with it is dry, and keep the box out of the rain on the way to the counter.
  • Pack for the long haul. Australia Post's guidance is box-in-box: a rigid carton that fits the card snugly, cushioned inside a second carton. Sendle's is an anti-static bag, 7cm or more of cushioning, then a shake to confirm nothing moves.
  • Pick a carrier that suits the NT and the card. Aramex does not operate in the Northern Territory. CouriersPlease's included cover excludes electronic devices. Sendle includes $100 of cover and sells more up to $1,500, so a dearer card is better served by Australia Post Extra Cover, which reaches $5,000.
  • Card only, in a plain carton. Cooler fitted, no cables, brackets or retail box, and the job number inside. Aramex's advice is to avoid any box that advertises electronics, and a “Fragile” sticker is no guarantee of gentle handling.

Bought it recently: claim from the seller first

On a recent card bought from a business, the seller is your first stop, not us. Consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law sit above any manufacturer's warranty and can run beyond it. You claim through the seller, who is not permitted to send you off to the manufacturer, and when the failure is major, refund or replacement is your call. In the ACCC's Federal Court action against Apple (2018, $9 million in penalties), the finding was that an outside repair cannot, on that ground alone, end your guarantees. The maker's warranty is its own arrangement, though, so lodge the claim before anyone opens the card. Private-sale cards and misuse damage fall outside the guarantees; that is where mail-in repair fits.

Questions

Does humidity damage graphics cards?

Rarely while the card is running, because a working card sits warmer than the air around it. The risk comes from moisture mixing with dust in the cooler, from a PC left off for weeks during the wet, and from condensation when a cold card meets humid air. Over time that can corrode contacts and components. Darwin's 3pm humidity sits between 65 and 72 per cent from December to February, so store spare cards sealed and dry. The diagnostic checks for corrosion under magnification.

Can lightning kill a graphics card?

Yes. Darwin Airport averages 13.4 lightning flashes per square kilometre each year, most between November and April, and a surge that reaches the PC can burn out the power stages on the card. Those are replaceable, from $430, once the diagnostic confirms that is the fault. Check whether the power supply survived as well. Power and Water does not pay for lightning or storm damage, so pulling the plug when a storm approaches is a habit worth having.

How long does post take from Darwin to Sydney?

On Australia Post's estimates, Express Post takes three to four business days and Parcel Post four to seven, with the return trip similar. Darwin sits outside the Express Post next-day guarantee, so neither figure is promised. For a thermal service, Express both ways works out at 7 to 10 business days door to door; a power stage repair sent by Parcel Post is 11 to 21. Public holidays and the time you take to approve the quote add to that.

Is there graphics card repair in Darwin?

Yes. At least one Darwin workshop advertises board-level graphics card repair, offers free diagnosis and takes postal jobs, and keeping the card local saves a long round trip. The other local results are general computer shops and national chains. Whoever you consider, compare the price range, the warranty term and what you pay if the card cannot be fixed. Our numbers: repairs from $330, a year's warranty on parts and labour, and a $150 diagnostic plus return postage if the card proves unrepairable.

My card is only a year old. Should I post it to you?

Not yet. A year-old card bought from a business should go back to that seller first. The consumer guarantees apply on top of any warranty and can outlast it, the seller has to handle the claim instead of sending you to the manufacturer, and a major failure lets you choose a refund or a replacement. Post it to us if it came from a private sale, was damaged by misuse, or the claim was refused.
