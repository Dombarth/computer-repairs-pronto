You can post the card to us, or bring it down yourself: Newcastle is roughly two hours away by road. We find the one part that gave out and replace it; the rest of the card carries on.

We have no premises in Newcastle. Hunter weather and the faults it leaves

At the Bureau of Meteorology's Newcastle Nobbys Signal Station, January averages a 25.6 °C maximum and 72% humidity at 3pm. Nobbys is right on the coast, so suburbs further inland run warmer; even so, it logs 14.9 days a year of 30 °C or above, and it hit 42.5 °C on 18 January 2013. Heat slowly dries out the paste and pads that carry warmth off the chips, and dust clogging the cooler adds to it. Damp, salty air near the beaches is hard on electronics in general. An overheating card usually needs a thermal service, from $330.

In June 2007 the storm during which the bulk carrier Pasha Bulker ran aground at Nobbys Beach dropped more than 300 mm of rain on the Hunter and left more than 105,000 homes across the storm region without power. When the East Coast Low of April 2015 arrived, roughly 240,000 Ausgrid customers were cut off at its peak, and Newcastle and the Upper and Lower Hunter were among the worst hit. A surge can destroy a card's power stages; repairing those starts from $430. Storm season, according to the SES, is September to April, and its advice is to unplug computers and gaming consoles before the weather turns.

Ausgrid is the Hunter's network. Its compensation covers damage from an incident on its own network, rules out events outside its control, has a $5,000 ceiling, wants evidence such as photos and repair reports, and points you to your insurer first. What we email after the bench test, the fault found plus a costed quote, is the kind of evidence insurers and Ausgrid both ask for, though no one can promise a claim will succeed. If Ausgrid says no, EWON can review it.

Sending a card from the Hunter

Maitland, Cessnock and Port Stephens: add a day. NSW Other postcodes are estimated at up to three business days by Express into Sydney, and up to two for the return.

Photograph it before it goes in the box. If a storm caused the damage, Ausgrid lists photos among the proof it wants.

Pack it the way the carriers suggest. Australia Post: one snug, rigid box inside another, cushioned between, with no faith in a "Fragile" label. Sendle: anti-static bag, at least 7cm of padding, then shake it to check nothing moves. Aramex: a plain outer box that does not advertise electronics.

Lodge over the counter. Keep the receipt, and something proving the card's value. Australia Post claims need both, have a six-month limit, and are not paid on an item that was not adequately packed.

The hub's packing guide has the full checklist.

A tip for Newcastle: if you use a courier rather than Australia Post, check the cover. Sendle includes $100 and sells more up to $1,500. CouriersPlease's included cover does not extend to electronic devices.

Newer cards and your rights

Under the Australian Consumer Law, a warranty is an extra layered over the consumer guarantees and cannot take them away. When a new card suffers a major failure, you decide between a refund and a replacement, and you claim from the business that sold it, which must deal with you itself instead of pointing at the maker. Cards damaged by misuse or bought privately are outside the guarantees. The Federal Court's 2018 judgment in ACCC v Apple, with $9 million in penalties, held that fixing a product through someone else does not by that fact cancel them. Even so, if your card is recent, go to the retailer or the warranty first, not to us.

Questions

There is a board-level repairer in Newcastle. Why would I post my card to Sydney?

You may not need to. If the local workshop can fix your card and its terms suit you, staying in Newcastle saves the postage. The differences are on paper: we publish a from-price for each type of repair, put 12 months of cover on the repair, and accept cards by post. It publishes no GPU prices or warranty term. Ask it about both before deciding either way.

How many days does the post take between Newcastle and your workshop?

Australia Post estimates one to two business days by Express from Newcastle 2300 to our Sydney Metro postcode, and one day for the return. Maitland 2320 and Cessnock are a zone further out: Express is estimated at up to three days in and up to two back. Parcel Post is three to four days each way from anywhere in the Hunter. None of those times is guaranteed.

Can a Hunter storm kill a graphics card?

It can. A surge during a storm can take out the power stages that feed the graphics processor, leaving a card that will not start, or one whose fans spin up before it cuts out. Power stage work starts from $430. Unplug the PC before severe weather, as NSW SES advises, and check your home and contents policy first; our written diagnosis records what failed.

My card's 12VHPWR plug has melted. Is it finished?

Usually not. A melted 12VHPWR or 12V-2x6 power connector is connector work, from $370: the damaged plug is removed, the surrounding board is inspected for heat damage, and a fresh connector is fitted. If the damage reaches further than the plug, the diagnostic will find it and the quote will say so before you commit to anything. Post the card without its power cable.

Is repairing a dead card better value than replacing it?

The answer turns on the card's value and on what failed. A thermal service from $330 makes sense on most cards. Power stage and memory faults, from $430 and from $530 respectively, suit mid-range cards and up, such as GeForce RTX 30-series and later, or Radeon RX 6000 and later. On a cheap older card, the diagnostic plus postage can exceed its value, and we will tell you so on the phone.
