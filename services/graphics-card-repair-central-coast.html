Central Coast GPU & Graphics Card Repair, Posted or Dropped Off

Yes, a graphics card (GPU) from the Central Coast can be repaired, and there are two ways to get it to us. Post it, or drive it to our workshop close to Castle Hill, about 53 minutes from Gosford when the roads are clear. Either way the card gets component-level repair: we trace the failed part on the board and replace that, instead of writing off the whole card.

We are not on the Coast, and there is no shopfront or collection point in Gosford, Erina or Wyong. What there is: a Hills District bench that has done board work since 2006, repairs from $330, a $150 diagnostic you do not pay if the repair goes ahead, and a year of warranty covering the parts and the labour. Our Australia-wide graphics card repair page sets out the mail-in process in full; below is what is specific to sending from the Coast.

Coast summers, East Coast Lows and your card

The Bureau of Meteorology's Gosford (Narara Research Station) records, which run to 2013, count 33.4 days a year that hit 30 °C, 7.6 that hit 35 °C, and a record 44.8 °C on 18 January 2013. Years of heat parch the thermal paste and pads sitting on the chips, and dust packed into the cooler fins makes it worse. The usual result is a card that throttles, roars its fans or crashes in games while seeming fine on the desktop: a thermal service, from $330.

Storm season, by the NSW SES reckoning, is September through April, and its 2023 hazard report squeezes roughly nine-tenths of NSW's yearly severe thunderstorm activity into October–March. In the April 2015 East Coast Low, about 240,000 Ausgrid customers were without power at the peak, and Ausgrid listed the Central Coast among the hardest-hit areas. A surge can kill the power stages that supply the graphics processor, which is a power stage repair, from $430. The SES tells households to pull the plug on computers and gaming consoles before severe weather arrives, and Ausgrid suggests surge protector power boards.

Ausgrid supplies the Coast. It considers claims for damage from an incident on its network, excludes events outside its control, caps claims at $5,000, asks for proof such as repair reports, and recommends speaking to your insurer first. For storm damage, home and contents insurance is usually where to begin. Our emailed diagnosis and quote name the failed part and the cost of fixing it, which is the evidence an insurer or Ausgrid will want to see. Whether a claim is paid is their decision, not ours. If a network refuses one, EWON, the Energy and Water Ombudsman NSW, is able to look at it again.

Posting tips for Gosford, Wyong and the peninsula

  • Keep the box out of a hot car. In summer, take it straight into the Post Office, not onto a back seat for the day.
  • Two boxes, one card. Australia Post wants a snug, rigid box packed inside a bigger one with cushioning; a "Fragile" sticker is no substitute. Sendle's tips add an anti-static bag, no less than 7cm of cushioning, and a shake of the sealed box.
  • Nothing but the card. Cooler fitted and the job number inside; leave the cables, brackets and retail box on the Coast.
  • Let it warm up when it comes back. Gosford's July mean minimum is 4.7 °C, and moving cold hardware into a warm room can cause condensation, so give the card time to reach room temperature before powering it.

Every packing step, down to the note that goes in the box, is on the mail-in hub.

Before you post: hang on to the Post Office receipt plus anything that shows what the card is worth. An Australia Post claim needs both and must be made within six months, and nothing is paid if the packing was not adequate. Sendle's price covers $100 and it will sell you cover to $1,500; CouriersPlease's included cover excludes electronic devices.

If the card is still new, start with the retailer

A card bought new from an Australian business comes with consumer guarantees. The maker's warranty is an addition to them, never a replacement. For a major failure you pick a refund or a replacement and deal with the shop that sold it; the shop must handle it rather than refer you to the maker. Misuse and private sales fall outside. A recently bought card belongs back with the retailer, or under its warranty, before anyone repairs it. Apple's 2018 loss to the ACCC in the Federal Court brought $9 million in penalties and a finding that repair by an outside party is not, in itself, the end of the guarantees. How a manufacturer treats its own warranty after a repair is another matter.

Questions

Is Express Post next day from the Central Coast?

Not with any guarantee. Coast postcodes fall outside the zone where Express Post comes with a next-business-day guarantee, so the next-day promise and its postage refund do not apply to a parcel from Gosford, Woy Woy or Wyong. Australia Post's estimate for Express into our Sydney Metro postcode is one to two business days, and one day for the trip back. Parcel Post is estimated at three to four days each way.

Can I drive my card down from Gosford instead of posting it?

Yes, by appointment, any day but Sunday, 8am to 6pm. Call 0400 454 859 or use the form, and we email the workshop address along with a job number. Gosford to Castle Hill is about 53 minutes in light traffic and longer at peak times. A card carried in pays the same as a posted one, gets the same $150 diagnostic, and carries the same 12 months of warranty.

Is anyone on the Central Coast repairing graphics cards at board level?

Our September 2026 search found no Coast business publishing a graphics card repair service, price or warranty. The shops that rank do general repairs, builds and upgrades, which is the right help for a software fault or a new card fitted. For a card with a failed component, the practical options are a Sydney workshop or the post. Ask anyone you consider whether they replace parts or swap the card.

Does summer heat on the Coast shorten a graphics card's life?

Heat is hard on the thermal paste and pads that move heat from the chips into the cooler. As they dry out, the card runs hotter and begins to throttle or crash under load. Gosford's Bureau of Meteorology records average about 33 days a year reaching 30 °C. The fix, a thermal service from $330 that replaces the paste and pads, usually needs a day or two of bench time.

What if the card turns out to be unfixable?

Then your cost is the $150 diagnostic and the postage home, nothing else. You are also told what failed, which is useful if the damage followed a storm and you are claiming on home and contents insurance. If a card looks unlikely to be worth the diagnostic in the first place, we will say so on the phone before you send it.
