How to tell if it is the laptop screen or something else

Connect the laptop to a TV or monitor: if the picture is fine there, the fault is in the laptop screen or its cable; if it is wrong there too, the fault is on the motherboard or graphics side. Press Windows + P and choose Duplicate. The external screen bypasses the laptop panel, so this one test splits the job in two.

  • Dell laptops have a built-in screen test. Turn the laptop off, hold the D key and press the power button. The panel fills with solid colours. Lines, dots or dark patches during the test point to the screen hardware; a clean test points to drivers or Windows. Dell's steps: Dell LCD built-in self-test.
  • Black screen but the laptop is on: press Windows + Ctrl + Shift + B to restart the graphics driver (you hear a beep and the screen flashes). If it stays dark, try the torch check in the table below.
  • Flicker: open Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc). If Task Manager flickers too, Microsoft says a display driver is the likely cause; if it stays steady, an app is. See Microsoft's flicker guide.

Next, find out which panel you have. Windows shows the resolution and the refresh rate (how many times a second the picture updates, in Hz) under Settings > System > Display > Advanced display (Microsoft's guide). The exact part number is on a label on the back of the panel, which we read once the screen is out.

Cracked laptop screen, lines, flicker or black screen: what each means

Cracks and ink-like blots mean a damaged panel; lines, flicker and a dark screen can be the panel, the cable or the backlight, and simple checks tell them apart.

What affects the price and time of a laptop screen repair

The price depends on which panel fits your laptop, what else broke in the same drop, and whether the part is in stock.

  • The panel part number must match. Size, resolution, connector (30 or 40 pins, and its position), refresh rate, touch and mounting (adhesive pull tabs on most modern panels, side screws or built-on brackets on older ones) all have to match. One laptop model can ship with several panels: Lenovo's spec sheet for the ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 12 lists five, from a 60Hz 1920 x 1200 IPS screen to a 120Hz 2880 x 1800 touch OLED. So we order by the part number on the panel label, not just the laptop model. The first letters name the panel maker: LP for LG Display, B for AU Optronics, LTN for Samsung, NV for BOE.
  • Bezel and adhesive. On many slim laptops the bezel (the plastic frame around the screen) is held by adhesive and clips, and the panel by stretch-release adhesive. iFixit's guide for the HP 15-fc0000 series warns the bezel is thin and breaks easily. A cracked bezel or lid is quoted as an extra part.
  • Hinges. A drop that breaks a screen can also crack a hinge or its mounts. We check and quote both before starting. Hinge repair is from $229.
  • 2-in-1 laptops. These often take a full display assembly, and on some the base comes apart first: iFixit's guide for the Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5-1570 removes the motherboard and battery before the display.
  • Parts in stock or ordered in. Some jobs can be done the same day when the panel is in stock. If it has to be ordered, you get a timeframe with the quote.

Questions

How much does it cost to replace a laptop screen?

A standard 14 to 15.6 inch screen starts from $259 fitted. Touch, OLED, 4K and high-refresh panels are quoted by model. Call 02 8428 9224 with your model number for a price.

Why is my laptop screen black but the laptop is on?

Shine a torch at the screen: a faint image means the backlight is not lighting. No image at all: try an external monitor. Fine there means the screen or cable; nothing on either points to the board.

How do I fix lines on my laptop screen?

If the lines show in the maker's screen test or before Windows loads, they are hardware, not drivers. Lines that change as you move the lid point to the display cable; lines that stay put point to the panel.

Can a cracked laptop screen be repaired without replacing it?

No. A cracked panel is replaced, not repaired. On touch laptops with separate front glass, cracked glass over a working picture may only need the touch layer or assembly.

Can I upgrade my laptop screen to 144Hz or 4K?

Only if your model was sold with that panel option, and it may need a different cable. High-refresh and 4K panels use 40-pin connectors that do not fit the 30-pin cable of a 60Hz 1080p panel.

How long does a laptop screen replacement take?

Some jobs can be done the same day when the panel is in stock. If it has to be ordered in, you get a timeframe with the quote.

Is a broken laptop screen covered by warranty?

A crack is physical damage, which Dell, for one, says its standard warranty typically does not cover. Lines or a dead screen on a laptop that was never dropped may be a defect: try the retailer first, as Australian Consumer Law guarantees can apply after the warranty ends.
