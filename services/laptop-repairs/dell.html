Dell laptop won't turn on: read the battery light

If a dead Dell blinks its battery light amber then white, it is showing a diagnostic code for where startup stopped. Count the amber blinks, then the white. Examples from Dell's XPS guide: 2 amber and 1 white is a processor failure; 2 amber and 3 white is no memory detected; 3 amber and 5 white is a power rail failure (the board's power-up sequence failed). The G15 5515 uses the same scheme. Dell notes the code shows how far startup got, not the exact failed part. The full table is in our laptop won't turn on guide.

No light at all means power is not reaching the laptop or the charging port has failed (Dell's adapter guide). Try a known-good Dell adapter of the right wattage first. On the bench we check the adapter input, DC jack, charging circuit and power rails (the supply lines the board needs to start). No-power repairs start from $329.

Dell built-in diagnostics: SupportAssist pre-boot (ePSA)

If the laptop powers on, tap F12 at the Dell logo and choose Diagnostics to run Dell's built-in hardware test. It runs from the BIOS before Windows loads, so it works when Windows will not start. Older manuals call it ePSA (Enhanced Pre-boot System Assessment). A failed test shows an error code and a validation code.

Dell SupportAssist pre-boot diagnostics (ePSA)

  1. Shut the laptop down fully.
  2. Turn it on and tap F12 at the Dell logo until the One Time Boot Menu appears.
  3. Select Diagnostics. The quick test starts on its own.
  4. On some models, such as the Latitude E5570, holding Fn while pressing power also opens this menu.
  5. To test one part, press Esc and pick it. Thorough mode runs a longer test.
  6. Stay with it: the screen (LCD) test asks you to confirm what you see.

What it tests: The hardware Dell detects in your laptop, tested from the BIOS before Windows loads.

Tell us: Write down the error code (like 2000-0xxx), the validation code and your Service Tag.

Dell laptop not charging: adapter ID, USB-C and charge limits

Before paying for a charging repair, press F2 at power-on and check two things in the BIOS: that the AC adapter is recognised, and that no charge limit is set. Dell lists the adapter on the BIOS Overview screen. If it shows as Unknown or the wrong wattage, the laptop may charge slowly or not at all.

Barrel (round-pin) adapters

Dell chargers carry an ID chip that tells the BIOS the adapter model, and on barrel plugs the thin centre pin carries that signal. If the laptop cannot read it, the BIOS shows "AC Adapter = Unknown" and you may see "The AC power adapter wattage and type cannot be determined". Dell says this can slow the processor and stop the battery charging. Dell names the usual causes as the adapter (including a bent or broken centre pin), the DC-in port (the laptop's power socket) or the charging circuit on the motherboard.

USB-C models

Newer XPS, Latitude and Precision models charge through USB-C. A weak source brings up "You have plugged in a lower wattage power adapter, USB-C charging device, or power pass-through device". Docks and hubs keep some power: Dell's example is a 130 W adapter passing only 90 W through a dock, and some hubs do not charge at all. Test with the adapter plugged straight in. If it still fails, we check the USB-C port and the power delivery chip behind it (the chip that agrees the voltage with the charger).

Charge limits that look like a fault

In the BIOS or the Dell Power Manager app, Primarily AC mode never charges to 100% (Dell's battery guide says 50 to 80%), and Custom mode stops at a level you set. ExpressCharge charges faster, but Dell warns it may wear the battery faster. Choose Standard to charge to 100%. More checks are in our laptop not charging guide.

Check your Dell warranty and consumer guarantee first

If your Dell is under warranty or fairly new, go to Dell or the seller before paying for a repair. Find the Service Tag, a 7-character code, on the bottom cover, in the BIOS (F2, Overview screen), in the SupportAssist app, or in PowerShell with GWMI -Class Win32_Bios | select SerialNumber. Enter it on Dell Australia's warranty status page to see the status and expiry date.

Australian Consumer Law guarantees are separate from the warranty. They can still apply after it ends, for a reasonable time based on the product's type and price. The ACCC says to start with the business that sold it (ACCC: broken but out of warranty). If neither applies, ring us with the Service Tag.

Dell XPS, Inspiron, Latitude, Vostro and Precision repair notes

Each Dell family is built differently, which changes the labour and the part. These notes come from Dell's service manuals and spec pages, plus Notebookcheck for the Precision memory.

Pick your range

Dell XPS

Dell's premium thin-and-light range.

Memory soldered to the board
Dell lists the memory on the XPS 13 9305 and 9340 as integrated on the system board; the 9340 manual says it is not upgradeable. A memory fault is a board-level job. Quoted by model
Keyboard comes with the palm rest
On the XPS 13 9310, Dell's manual removes the battery, display and system board, leaving the palm-rest and keyboard assembly. From $229
Three screens on one model
The XPS 13 9340 shipped with Full HD+ non-touch, QHD+ touch or 2.8K OLED touch panels. We confirm yours from the Service Tag before ordering. Quoted by model

Dell Inspiron

Dell's mainstream home range (for example the Inspiron 15 3520).

Keyboard swap is a full teardown
Dell's 3520 manual removes 16 parts, including the system board, to reach the palm-rest and keyboard assembly. From $229
Internal battery
The 3-cell or 4-cell battery sits under the base cover. From $179
Power socket is a separate part
The 3520 manual lists the power-adapter port as its own part, so a damaged socket is a part swap. From $189

Dell Latitude

Dell's business range (for example the Latitude 5540).

Keyboard on its own bracket
On the Latitude 5540 the keyboard lifts off a bracket on the palm rest, so it is replaced on its own, but the system board comes out first. From $229
Docks and hubs cut charging power
Dell says a 130 W adapter passes only 90 W through a dock or hub. Test with the adapter plugged straight in before booking a port repair. From $189

Dell Vostro

Dell's small-business range. No Vostro-specific faults are well documented; these are notes from Dell's manual.

Same teardown as the Inspiron 15 3520
Dell's Vostro 3520 manual lists the same teardown steps as the Inspiron 15 3520: the keyboard comes with the palm rest and the battery is internal. From $229
Separate power socket and memory modules
Both are separate parts on the 3520, which keeps those repairs simpler. From $189

Dell Precision

Dell's mobile workstations.

CAMM memory (Precision 7670)
Notebookcheck reports the 7670 was the first laptop with Dell's CAMM memory: a flat module held by six screws instead of SODIMM sticks. An upgrade replaces the whole module. Quoted by model
High-wattage adapters
Dell adapters range from 65 W to 330 W; the wattage is on the label. A smaller one can stop the battery charging. From $189

How much does Dell laptop repair cost?

How much is a Dell laptop screen replacement?

From $259 for a standard 14 to 15.6 inch screen. Touch, OLED, 4K and high refresh panels are quoted by model, and the Service Tag tells us which one yours has.

Why won't my Dell laptop turn on?

Check the battery light. Amber-then-white blinks are a Dell diagnostic code; no light at all points to the adapter, DC jack or charging circuit.

Why is my Dell laptop plugged in but not charging?

Check the BIOS (F2 at power-on) first. Primarily AC or Custom charge mode stops short of 100%, and an adapter shown as Unknown points to the charger, its cable or the DC jack.

How do I check my Dell warranty in Australia?

Enter the 7-character Service Tag on Dell Australia's Support Services and Warranty page. If the laptop is covered, or recent enough for a consumer guarantee claim, go to Dell or the seller first.

Can you replace a Dell XPS battery?

Yes, from $179. If it is swollen, stop using the laptop and unplug the charger until it is replaced.

Is it worth repairing my Dell laptop?

It depends on the fault and the model. Phone advice is free; if the $150 bench diagnostic shows it is not worth fixing, we tell you why and you pay only the $150.
