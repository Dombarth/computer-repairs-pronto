Because the Tweed is inside the network, the next-business-day guarantee can apply, on these conditions:

Tweed weather, the 2022 flood and the faults they cause

The nearest long-record Bureau of Meteorology station is Coolangatta (040717), at Gold Coast Airport over the border, with records from 1982. January averages a 28.5 °C maximum and 69% humidity at 3pm (1993–2010), yet 35 °C days come to only 0.5 a year: humid, not hot. Years of warm running still dry a card's paste and pads, and dust packs the cooler; loud fans and sagging clocks call for a thermal service, from $330. Sea air and humidity are a general risk to coastal electronics. July nights average 10.1 °C, so let a cold-arriving card reach room temperature before switching on, or condensation can form.

The late-February 2022 flood was the Tweed's biggest. The Tweed River at Murwillumbah peaked at 6.51 m, 0.31 m over the 2017 record, and Tumbulgum reached 4.8 m, 800 mm above 2017. Bogangar, Tweed Heads South and Pottsville were affected, the council called power and internet patchy, and Essential Energy's Murwillumbah zone substation was among those hit. A PC that went under stays off until a licensed electrician says otherwise (the advice from NSW SES and Essential Energy), and a repair workshop cannot stand in for that electrician. Submerged cards are often not worth the repair bill; the diagnostic gives a straight answer.

Ex-Tropical Cyclone Alfred came next, in March 2025: on 7 March more than 38,000 homes and businesses were without power, worst between Tweed Heads and Yamba, and 84,000 lost supply in all between Tweed Heads and Grafton. Storm season, NSW SES says, lasts from September until April; before severe weather, unplug the PC and any gaming console. Surge damage to a card's power stages is repairable, from $430.

Essential Energy, the NSW-side distributor, generally does not reimburse storm damage and accepts liability only for its own negligence, so home and contents insurance is the usual first claim. Both ask for invoices, quotations or an electrician's report; the fault report and quote we email are that kind of paperwork. No outcome is promised, and EWON can review a refused NSW network claim. Queensland-side customers should ask their own distributor.

Posting tips for Tweed and Gold Coast senders

  • Express, over the counter. A day each way against two to four for Parcel Post, and weekday counter lodgement meets the guarantee's lodgement condition and gets you a receipt.
  • Pack for the trip. Australia Post suggests a snug, rigid box inside a second box with cushioning between; add Sendle's anti-static bag, 7 cm-plus of padding and shake test, and Aramex's plain, unbranded outer box. Cooler on, job number in, nothing else. "Fragile" labels are not fail-safe.
  • Wet-season wrapping. October to March, slip the parcel into a plastic bag for the trip to the Post Office.
  • PO Box returns. Returns can go to a PO Box, NSW or Queensland side. Include a prepaid label, or have return postage added to the invoice at cost.

The complete packing guide lives on the mail-in hub.

Recent cards: the retailer's job first

A recently bought card goes back to its store or online retailer first. A manufacturer's warranty adds to the Australian Consumer Law's guarantees and cannot take them away. The seller must handle your claim rather than refer you to the maker, and a major failure gives you the choice of refund or replacement; misuse and private sales are not covered. According to the Federal Court's 2018 ACCC v Apple ruling, repair by a third party does not in itself end the guarantees, although manufacturer warranties carry their own terms.

Questions

Is Express Post next day from Tweed Heads to Sydney?

On Australia Post's estimates, yes: one business day each way between Tweed Heads (2485) and our Sydney Metro postcode, and the Tweed is one of the few regional NSW areas inside the next-business-day Express network, so the guarantee can apply. Lodge at a counter Monday to Friday, or use a yellow Express box before it is cleared, and steer clear of red boxes and Parcel Lockers. If a guaranteed item is late, Australia Post refunds the postage. Check your postcode pair in its online calculator first.

I am in Coolangatta. What are your hours on Queensland time?

We answer 0400 454 859 from 8am to 6pm, Monday to Saturday, on Sydney time. Outside daylight saving that matches Queensland. From early October to early April, when NSW moves its clocks forward and Queensland does not, it is 7am to 5pm in Coolangatta. Tweed Heads keeps NSW time, so for Tweed callers the hours never change. The form works at any hour.

Can I send a card from the Gold Coast rather than the Tweed?

Yes. Gold Coast postcodes 4210 to 4275 share the Tweed's Express Post zone, put at a single business day per leg, and Parcel Post counts them as Brisbane Metro at two to four days. Prices, the $150 diagnostic and the warranty are exactly as they are for Tweed customers. Our Brisbane page covers Queensland customers in more detail, and the packing advice on this page applies on either side of the border.

Can a Tweed graphics card damaged by flood or storm be fixed?

Storm damage often can. When a surge gets through the PC's power supply it can kill the card's power stages, and those are replaced from $430. Flood is harder: cards that have sat under water are frequently uneconomic to fix, and any PC that went under should not be powered up before a licensed electrician has looked at it, per NSW SES and Essential Energy advice. The $150 diagnostic tells you which case yours is, in writing.

Can you post my repaired card to a PO Box in the Tweed?

Yes, returns to a PO Box are no problem, on the Tweed or Gold Coast side. Either put a prepaid return label in with the card, or let the return postage be added to your invoice at cost. You still choose the service and cover for the journey home, and the card is sent once the invoice is paid. Express keeps the return to an estimated single business day.
