Send it by post to our bench near Castle Hill, in north-west Sydney, and we repair it at component level: the power stage, memory chip or connector that failed is replaced, not the whole card.

We are based in Sydney, not Armidale or anywhere else on the Tablelands, and have done board work there since 2006. Prices start from $330. The diagnostic costs $150 and is dropped from the bill when you approve the repair, and the warranty on the replaced parts lasts 12 months, for a recurrence of the same fault. Cold, storms and New England power

The Bureau of Meteorology's Armidale Airport station (056238) records a July mean minimum of 1.4 °C and averages 32.5 days a year at 0 °C or below; its lowest reading was −7.0 °C, on 19 July 2007. At close to 1,000 metres, summer is mild by comparison: a January mean maximum of 26.4 °C, and 0.5 days a year reaching 35 °C. The card's own heat still dries its paste and pads over the years, whatever the weather outside.

Cold matters most when a card moves. One that has spent a July night in a car or a van can gather condensation when it comes into a heated room, so let it reach room temperature before it powers up.

Storms are the bigger threat to the electronics. At about 10 pm on 14 October 2021, a tornado tore roofs off, uprooted trees and brought down power lines, with North Hill badly hit; roughly half of Armidale lost power overnight, and Essential Energy reported many spans of line and several poles down. On 12 January 2025, lightning dropped a high-voltage line into the low-voltage network and cut supply to 1,140 homes in North Armidale. A surge from an event like either can kill the power stages on a graphics card, work we charge from $430 to repair. Storm season in NSW, by the SES's reckoning, runs from September to April, and the SES tells households to unplug computers and consoles when a severe storm is coming; Essential Energy agrees.

Essential Energy, the local distributor, compensates only for damage caused by its own negligence and treats storms and lightning as beyond its control. That usually makes your home and contents policy the place to claim first. Its claim form calls for invoices or quotes for repairs, or an electrician's report, and insurers want similar proof; our emailed diagnosis and fixed quote states what failed and what fixing it costs. If a network knocks a claim back, EWON can look at the decision again. We make no promise that any claim will be paid.

Sending a card from the Tablelands

  • Winter returns. When the card comes home in June or July, let the parcel warm up indoors before you open it.
  • Two boxes, a receipt and a record of value. Anti-static bag, snug rigid inner box, at least 7cm of cushioning inside a larger outer box, then a shake test; a "Fragile" sticker is no substitute, and a poorly packed card gets no payout. Lodge over a Post Office counter and keep the receipt and proof of value; any claim needs both, and has a six-month time limit.
  • Courier cover at this value. Sendle's extra cover tops out at $1,500 and CouriersPlease's standard cover excludes electronic devices, so at $4,000, of these three, only Australia Post's Extra Cover stretches far enough.
  • PO Box returns. A PO Box works as the return address just as well as a street address. Pay for the return with a prepaid label packed alongside the card, or at cost on our invoice.

Send only the card, with its cooler fitted, in a plain box; the retail carton, cables and bracket can stay in Armidale. For the full packing guide, see our mail-in hub.

Consumer rights come first on a new card

Buy a card new from a shop and the Australian Consumer Law gives you guarantees that no manufacturer's warranty can shrink; the warranty only adds to them. If the card has failed in a major way, the retailer owes you a refund or a replacement, your pick, and it cannot redirect you to the maker. Misuse and cards bought second-hand from a private seller are not covered. The ACCC's 2018 Federal Court win against Apple, with $9 million in penalties, established that an independent repair does not by itself wipe those rights out. Still, go to the retailer or warranty first.

Questions

Will Express Post get my card from Armidale to Sydney overnight?

Not with any guarantee. Australia Post's next-business-day promise holds only inside its Express network, and Armidale 2350 is outside it. Its estimate for Express from Armidale's NSW Other zone is one to three business days to Sydney and one to two coming back. Parcel Post takes an estimated three to four days either way. For a card you rely on every day, Express is still the quicker choice.

What does a graphics card repair cost from Armidale?

Exactly what a Sydney customer pays. Our lowest prices, from $330, cover a thermal service or a firmware recovery. Connectors are from $370, power stages from $430, memory chips from $530 and a reball from $630, which is quoted per card. If you go ahead, the $150 diagnostic comes off. Add postage both ways, plus Extra Cover if the card is worth more than $100; a card you value at $4,000 needs $97.50 of it per parcel.

My card has been in the cold overnight. Can I power it up straight away?

Wait until it has reached room temperature. When cold hardware comes into a warm room, moisture in the air can condense on the board, and switching on a damp card is an avoidable risk. With a July mean minimum of 1.4 °C at Armidale Airport, that applies to a parcel collected from the Post Office on a winter morning as much as to a card carried in from the car.

Can you send the repaired card to an Armidale PO Box?

Yes. We return cards to a PO Box or a street address, whichever you give us, which helps if nobody is home during the day. Include a prepaid return label with the card, or we add the return postage at cost to the invoice, and the card is dispatched once it is paid. Remember that Signature on Delivery is compulsory for anything valued over $500.

Can a card that is completely dead be brought back?

Often it can. A card with no sign of life may have a fault on its power side, such as a shorted power stage, and that part can be replaced. The diagnostic, $150 and waived if you proceed, is how we find out. Some cards cannot be saved, for example when the GPU itself has failed; then you pay the diagnostic and return postage, and we explain what went wrong.
