Before you drop off a work laptop

Get the BitLocker recovery key and tell us if the laptop is managed by your company, before it leaves the office.

  • BitLocker key: Microsoft lists a motherboard replacement and firmware updates among the events that can make Windows ask for the 48-digit recovery key. On a work laptop the key is usually held by your IT administrator, not the user.
  • Managed laptops: if your company manages the laptop (for example with Microsoft Intune), tell us. A Windows reinstall may need your administrator to enrol it again.
  • Back up: if it still runs, copy anything not already saved to your company's storage. Most repairs do not touch your files.
  • Bring the charger: we test with it, and docks can limit charging power.

No contracts or retainers

We do not sell managed-service contracts or monthly retainers: you ring when something breaks and pay for that job. That suits small businesses without an IT person who need a repair done properly. If you need ongoing support for servers, networks or many users on site, an IT services firm with site visits is the better fit.
