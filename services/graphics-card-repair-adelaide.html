GPU & Graphics Card Repair for Adelaide, by Mail-In

Yes, an Adelaide graphics card (GPU) can go in the post to our Sydney bench and be repaired at component level, at the same price we charge over the counter. Rather than declaring the whole card dead, we find the individual part that failed and replace it. Be aware before you start that we have no workshop in Adelaide or anywhere else in South Australia. All of the work is done in Sydney, at our workshop by Castle Hill, and the card travels by post in both directions.

Prices start from $330. A $150 bench diagnostic comes first and produces a fixed quote, and when you approve the repair that $150 is dropped from the invoice. Every part we replace then comes with 12 months' warranty, valid if the same fault shows up again. The general process for every state is on our Australia-wide graphics card mail-in page; the sections below deal with the Adelaide-specific parts: postage times, local options, climate and packing.

The post is quicker than the distance suggests: Express from Adelaide is estimated to reach Sydney in a single business day; Parcel Post takes an estimated two to four.

How the Express guarantee applies to Adelaide

Adelaide and Sydney are both capitals inside the Express Post network, and that network is where the next-business-day guarantee holds. Darwin is excluded from it altogether, and Perth has it for the CBD only. From Adelaide it applies to parcels handed over a Post Office counter from Monday to Friday before closing, or dropped in a yellow street box before its cut-off, often around 4pm though times vary. Use a Parcel Locker, or a red street box, and the guarantee is gone. If a guaranteed item turns up late, you get the postage refunded, and no compensation for the wait. Public holidays are not delivery days.

Adelaide heat and worn-out cooling

Adelaide summers run warm. Bureau of Meteorology mean maximum temperatures are 26.9°C in December, 28.7°C in January and 28.6°C in February, and a mean blends the hot days with the mild ones. A card in a warm room starts every session with less cooling headroom, so paste and pads that have worn out over the years begin to show it.

The symptoms: a hotspot temperature well above the core reading, fans spinning hard under light load, and frame rates sagging partway through a long session as the card throttles. A thermal service strips the cooler, cleans it, and fits fresh paste and pads of the correct thickness. Priced from $330, it is usually finished within one or two business days, which makes it among the cheapest and quickest jobs on our list.

Coloured blocks, lines or wrong textures are a separate problem, usually memory, or something outside the card such as a cable or driver. Our guide to GPU artefacting takes you through the checks before you post.

Packing and posting from Adelaide

  • Lean towards Express for valuable cards. Parcel Post from Adelaide is estimated at up to four business days each way, so Express can cut up to three days from each leg and adds the guarantee.
  • Pack in two layers. Australia Post's advice is to pack box-in-box: the card goes in a snug, rigid box, which then sits inside a larger one lined with cushioning. Sendle's advice adds three things: an anti-static bag around the card, cushioning no thinner than 7cm, and a shake test once the box is sealed.
  • Keep it anonymous. Use a plain outer box, not one printed with electronics branding, and leave the retail box, cables and brackets in Adelaide. The cooler stays on the card.
  • Have your paperwork ready. Hold on to the lodgement receipt, plus something that shows the card's value, such as the invoice or a record of what you paid. Loss claims come from the sender, claims have a six-month limit, and a card judged inadequately packed gets nothing from Australia Post.

Worth knowing: if you use a courier instead, check the cover. Sendle's price includes $100 of cover, with more available up to a ceiling of $1,500. CouriersPlease's built-in cover does not extend to electronic devices, which rules it out as protection for a graphics card.

Consumer guarantees come before repair

A card bought new from a business that has failed through no misuse on your part gives you rights against the seller. A manufacturer's warranty is extra to those rights, never a substitute, and the rights do not necessarily end when the warranty does. Go to the store first; for a major failure, you decide whether it is a refund or a replacement. Cards from a private sale fall outside the guarantees. On repairs, the ACCC took Apple to the Federal Court, which in 2018 imposed $9 million in penalties and found that a repair carried out by another party does not, on that ground alone, bring the consumer guarantees to an end. What a manufacturer does with its own warranty after an outside repair is a different question, so on any recent card, start with the retailer or the warranty.

Questions

What are the signs of a dead GPU?

The usual ones are a black screen with the card fitted, fans that spin up and then stop, a PC that will not start until the card is removed, coloured blocks or lines already visible at the boot screen, and a burning smell. Each of these can come from a single failed part, so none of them proves the card is finished. Before posting from Adelaide, try another cable, monitor and slot, because they can cause similar symptoms.

Is a graphics card under $1,000 worth repairing?

It can be, depending on the fault. A thermal service from $330 or a connector repair from $370 can make sense on a card worth $700 or $800, even after postage both ways. A memory or reball job on the same card is harder to justify. At the bottom of the market, a card that would only fetch around $200 used seldom covers the diagnostic and two lots of postage. Ring before posting and we will run the numbers with you.

How do I uncorrupt a GPU?

First work out where the corruption lives. If the picture is clean at the boot screen and only breaks inside Windows or one game, remove the graphics driver completely and reinstall it. If corruption appears before Windows loads, the fault is hardware, often memory. If the card went dark after a BIOS flash, a dual-BIOS switch may bring it back; without one, firmware recovery starts from $330. Our GPU artefacting guide covers the full sequence.

How long does a mail-in repair take from Adelaide?

Add up three stretches. Getting to Sydney is estimated at one business day by Express Post or two to four by Parcel Post, and coming home takes about the same. In between, a repaste or firmware fix needs one or two business days, a power stage or memory job needs three to seven, and a reball or physically damaged card needs five to ten. Send a thermal job Express both ways and it can be home in under a week.

What does GPU repair cost in Adelaide?

Exactly what a Sydney walk-in pays. A thermal service or a firmware recovery starts from $330, connector and port work from $370, power stage repairs from $430, GDDR memory replacement from $530, and from $630 for reflow or reball. The $150 diagnostic is waived once you approve the repair, and postage each way is yours to choose and pay. An unfixable card costs the $150 plus return postage, nothing else.
