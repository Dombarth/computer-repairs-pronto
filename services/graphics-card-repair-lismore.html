Flood, heat and outages in Lismore

2022 and the water

The February 2022 flood was a disaster for Lismore. On 28 February the Wilsons River peaked at about 14.4 metres, more than two metres over the 1954 benchmark and far above the 11.6 metres that topped the levee in 2017. Across the region, 4,055 properties were assessed as uninhabitable and 13 people died. Essential Energy counted 69,603 customers hit by outages; the South Lismore zone substation stopped working entirely, and the CBD's underground network was under water for days.

If a computer went under, please leave it off. NSW SES and Essential Energy both want anything that has been in floodwater inspected by a licensed electrician before it is used, and no repair bench stands in for that. Cards that spent time submerged are frequently beyond a sensible repair; the diagnostic will say which yours is.

Heat and humidity

Lismore runs hotter than the coast. Lismore Airport AWS (station 058214, 2002–2026) averages 30.2 °C in January, hits 35 °C on 11.1 days a year, and set its 42.8 °C record on 12 February 2017. The older Centre Street station (058037) puts January humidity at 74% at 9am and 58% at 3pm. In a hot, shut-up room a card has little cooling headroom; summer after summer its paste and pads dry out, and dust in the fins adds to it. Roaring fans at the desktop or frame rates that sag on hot afternoons point to a thermal service, from $330. July mornings average 6.2 °C, though, so let a card that arrived cold warm up before power-up, since condensation forms when cold hardware meets warm air.

Storms, outages and claims

On 8 July 2024 a transmission line near Lismore tripped, and that evening Essential Energy was directed to shed load: about 24,500 customers in Lismore, Ballina and nearby areas were off from about 6:34pm until close to 10pm. NSW SES says to switch off and unplug equipment once power is lost and, with severe weather on the way, to disconnect computers and gaming consoles. A surge can wreck a card's power stages; that repair is from $430.

Essential Energy, the distributor, does not generally reimburse storm damage and pays only for its own negligence, so begin with your home and contents insurer. Either will want paperwork (Essential Energy lists an electrician's report, quotes or repair invoices), and the diagnosis and quote we email supply the fault and the figure. The outcome is theirs to decide; EWON can review a network claim Essential Energy turns down.

Packing and lodging in Lismore

  1. Beat the heat. On a 35-degree afternoon a parked car gets far hotter than the card's usual room; go straight to the counter.
  2. Waterproof the walk. October to March brings most severe storms, so carry the sealed box in a plastic bag until it is over the counter.
  3. Two boxes, no rattle. Anti-static bag, at least 7 cm of cushioning (Sendle's number), a snug rigid box inside an outer box (Australia Post's method), then a shake test. "Fragile" stickers promise nothing; Aramex prefers plain boxes that do not advertise electronics. Card only, cooler on, job number in; leads, brackets and the retail box stay home.
  4. Choose the return address. If street delivery out towards Nimbin, The Channon or Dunoon is unreliable, give us a PO Box. Add a prepaid return label, or we invoice return postage at cost.

When the retailer should hear about it first

A recently bought card belongs back with its retailer first. Under the Australian Consumer Law your guarantees stand apart from any warranty, which cannot pare them back. The seller deals with it and may not palm you off on the manufacturer; after a major failure, refund or replacement is your call. Misuse and private purchases sit outside. On third-party repair, the Federal Court in ACCC v Apple (2018) held that it does not by itself end the guarantees; a manufacturer's warranty may have its own conditions.

Questions

Is a graphics card from a flooded Lismore house worth repairing?

Often not, if it sat in floodwater, but only a diagnostic can say for certain. Before anything else, do not power up a computer that went under. NSW SES and Essential Energy want anything flood-affected looked at by a licensed electrician before use, and our bench test is not that check. If you still want the card assessed, the emailed result names what failed and whether repair makes sense, which is the sort of record an insurer asks for.

How many days does posting a GPU from Lismore to Sydney take?

By Australia Post's estimates, Express Post from Lismore's NSW Other zone reaches our Sydney Metro postcode in one to three business days and comes back in one to two. Parcel Post takes three to four each way. Neither is guaranteed, since 2480 lies outside the Express Post next-business-day network. Sent Express both ways, a repaste comes to three to seven business days all up, plus your time approving the quote.

Do Lismore summers wear out graphics cards faster?

Heat bakes the thermal paste and pads sitting between the GPU, memory and cooler, and they dry out with time. Lismore Airport averages 30.2 °C in January and logs 35-degree days 11.1 times a year, so a PC in a warm room without air conditioning works its cooler harder than one on the coast. Loud fans and falling clock speeds are the usual signs, and a thermal service starts from $330.

Who can fix a dead graphics card in Lismore?

We did not find a Lismore business advertising component-level GPU repair. Local results are general computer shops, onsite technicians and a national network, and none publish a GPU price or warranty. A local shop is still handy for testing the card in another PC. For the repair itself, you can post it down to us near Castle Hill: repairs from $330, a $150 diagnostic that comes off the bill when you proceed, and twelve months' warranty.

Is my Lismore graphics card worth repairing, or should I replace it?

Weigh our quote against what an equivalent card costs. Recent GeForce and Radeon generations, the RTX 30 to 50 series and RX 6000 to 9000, are where a power stage job from $430 looks small next to a replacement. An older card that resells for two or three hundred dollars is another story: the $150 diagnostic plus two legs of postage from the Northern Rivers can swallow most of its value, and we will say so before you post it.
