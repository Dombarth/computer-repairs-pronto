Computer Repairs for Blacktown, Mount Druitt and the North-West

Ring 0400 454 859 first: quotes over the phone cost nothing, and we tell you if a job is not worth doing before you get in the car.

Still on Windows 10? Your deadline is now October 2027

Microsoft stopped standard support for Windows 10 on 14 October 2025. Home PCs enrolled in its Extended Security Updates program keep receiving security fixes, and in June 2026 Microsoft pushed the end of that program back a year, to 12 October 2027. If your PC was already enrolled, the extra year applies automatically.

Enrolment is done from Windows Update. It is free if you back up your PC settings to a Microsoft account, or costs 1,000 Microsoft Rewards points, or a one-off payment of US$30 or the local equivalent. After October 2027 a Windows 10 PC keeps working, but every new security flaw stays open, which is a poor bet for a computer you bank or shop on.

Before you replace a Windows 10 PC, find out why it cannot move to Windows 11. Microsoft's free PC Health Check app gives the answer. The usual hurdles are TPM 2.0, Secure Boot and the processor, which generally has to be an Intel 8th generation or AMD Ryzen 2000 series or newer.

Tools that force Windows 11 onto unsupported hardware do exist. Microsoft does not guarantee updates on those machines, so we do not suggest them for a PC you rely on. If the answer is a new computer, the old drive usually still holds everything you need, and getting the data off it is a job we do.

Gaming PCs, graphics cards and consoles

A gaming PC that crashes, restarts or loses its picture under load is where guessing gets expensive. The same symptom can come from the power supply, the graphics card, overheating or a driver, so we test before anyone buys a part.

  • Crashes or black screens only in games: often a power supply that cannot hold up under load, or a failing power stage on the graphics card.
  • Coloured blocks, lines or no display: usually the graphics card itself. We repair cards at component level, with graphics card repair from $330 for thermal work and from $430 for power stage faults.
  • Fans at full speed and high temperatures: dried thermal paste or pads and a clogged cooler, which a thermal service sorts out.
  • PlayStation, Xbox or Switch faults: HDMI ports, power supplies and overheating on PS4 and PS5, Xbox One and Series consoles, and the Nintendo Switch. See console repairs.

Want more speed rather than a repair? Our upgrades page covers SSD, RAM, graphics card and processor upgrades for desktops.

Questions

Is there a computer repair shop in Blacktown?

There are walk-in shops in Blacktown itself, and for quick software jobs they are handy. We are not one of them: our workshop is near Castle Hill, about 20 to 25 minutes away outside peak times. People from Blacktown bring us the jobs that need a bench, such as dead laptops, liquid damage, graphics card faults and data recovery. The phone quote is free either way.

Can my computer be upgraded to Windows 11?

Run Microsoft's free PC Health Check app. If it passes, the upgrade is free through Windows Update, and a slow machine usually just needs an SSD and more RAM first. If it fails on TPM or Secure Boot, the fix is often a firmware setting. If it fails on the processor, a laptop cannot be brought up to standard, while a desktop may take a newer motherboard and processor.

What happens to Windows 10 after October 2027?

Home PCs enrolled in Microsoft's Extended Security Updates receive security fixes until 12 October 2027, after Microsoft extended the program by a year in June 2026. After that date, Windows 10 keeps running but gets no more security fixes, so new flaws stay open. For a PC used for banking, shopping or work email, plan to move it to Windows 11 or replace it before then.

What is the average cost to fix a PC?

There is no useful average, because a loose cable and a failed motherboard are both called a broken PC. Our phone quote is free, and when the fault needs finding on the bench the hands-on diagnostic is $150, waived if you go ahead with the repair. Graphics card repairs start from $330. For other parts, the price depends on the exact part your machine takes.

Is it worth fixing a broken laptop?

A rough rule: fix it if the repair costs well under half the price of a comparable new laptop and the machine can run Windows 11 or a current macOS. A cracked screen or a failed charging port on a three-year-old laptop is usually worth doing. A board fault on a laptop that cannot run Windows 11 usually is not, and we will say so on the phone.

Do you repair PS5 and Xbox consoles?

Yes. We repair PS4 and PS5 consoles, Xbox One and Xbox Series S and X, and the Nintendo Switch. HDMI port replacement is one of the most common console jobs, along with power supply faults and overheating. Consoles are drop-off at the same workshop near Castle Hill, and you can ring 0400 454 859 for a quote before you bring one in.
