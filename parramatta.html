Before you bring it in

  • Back up if you still can. If the computer still starts, copy anything you cannot replace to an external drive or cloud storage first. A repair should not touch your files, but a failing drive can stop at any moment.
  • Find your BitLocker recovery key. Many Windows 11 laptops encrypt the drive automatically. After a motherboard repair, a BIOS update or a drive transfer, Windows can ask for the 48-digit recovery key before it will start. If you sign in with a Microsoft account, the key is usually saved under Devices at account.microsoft.com. Find it before the repair, not after.
  • Bring the charger with a laptop. A failed charger or cable can look exactly like a dead laptop, and we need yours to rule it out.
  • Bring only the tower with a desktop. Leave the monitor, keyboard and mouse at home unless one of them is the problem.
  • Write down the password, or set up a spare account. We need to sign in to test the machine properly once it is fixed.
  • Note what happens and when. Error messages, beeps, flashing lights, and whether it fails from cold or only once it warms up, all shorten the diagnosis. A photo of the screen helps.
  • Spilt something on it? Switch it off, unplug it, do not try to charge it and do not put it in rice. Corrosion keeps working on the board while it sits, so bring it in as soon as you can.

Questions

How much does it cost to fix a computer in Parramatta?

It depends on the fault. The phone quote is free, and for common jobs we can give you a from-price on the call. When the fault has to be found on the bench, the hands-on diagnostic is $150, waived if you go ahead with the repair. Published starting prices include graphics card repair from $330 and Intel iMac SSD upgrades from $599. Laptop screens and batteries are quoted by model, because the part sets the price.

Is it worth repairing a computer?

Usually, if one part has failed and the machine still does what you need. A board-level repair often costs a fraction of a new laptop or motherboard. It is not worth it when the repair costs more than the machine would sell for and it is already too slow for your work. We tell you which side of that line yours is on during the phone quote, before you drive anywhere.

Do you have a shop in Parramatta?

No. Our workshop is near Castle Hill in the Hills District, about 20 to 25 minutes' drive from Parramatta outside peak hour. Repairs are drop-off: ring 0400 454 859 for a quote and the drop-off details, bring the computer in between 8am and 6pm, Monday to Saturday, and collect it when we call to say it is ready.

Do you come to homes in Parramatta?

Home computer repairs are done at our workshop, where the diagnostic and soldering equipment is. For software-only jobs, such as removing a virus or setting up a printer, a mobile technician who comes to you may suit you better. Businesses can ask about our business IT support, where we come to the office if a visit is needed.

Can you fix a laptop with water damage?

Often, if it is dealt with quickly. Switch it off, unplug it and do not try to charge it or turn it on to check. Rice does not dry out a circuit board. Liquid leaves residue and corrosion on the board, which we clean and then repair at component level, replacing the parts that have failed. The sooner it reaches the bench, the better the odds.

Can you recover files from a laptop that will not turn on?

Often the drive is fine even when the laptop is dead, so we can either repair the laptop or recover the files from the drive. Standard data recovery jobs are no data, no fee. Laptops with storage soldered to the board, such as recent MacBooks, need the board working again first, which is where board-level repair comes in.
